## When to submit (read first)

**Do not submit until there are 3+ paying customers willing to leave
honest reviews.** G2 rejects single-customer listings and tracks
incentivised-review patterns aggressively. The reputation cost of
getting caught farming reviews is catastrophic and forever.

The strategic moment to submit G2 is: the Founding Cohort has 3-5 named
paying customers who have run the Playbook for at least 14 days, and
the operator has direct relationships with them (Slack thread, email
chain) such that asking for an honest review – including critical
feedback – is natural.

**Brunson Hard-Rule, restated for this directory**: the listing is the
ask, not the review. The review request is a separate, honest, one-time
note to each customer. No discounts in exchange for reviews. No
"please be positive" framing. The G2 review prompt itself is sent by
G2, not by the operator.

## Verification

G2 requires verification that the submitter represents the vendor.
Steps:

1. Upload company logo + screenshots.
2. Verify domain ownership – G2 will ask for a DNS TXT record or a
   meta tag. Drop the meta tag in `app/src/app/layout.tsx`
   `verification` block via the existing buildVerification helper
   pattern (operator may need to add an env slot for `G2_VERIFICATION`
   if not already declared).
3. Optionally connect Salesforce or HubSpot for review-syndication –
   skip for now, neither is wired.

## After approval

1. Copy the public canonical URL (looks like

   vercel env add NEXT_PUBLIC_UNLOCKSAAS_G2_URL production
