## 1. Marc Lou — ShipFast (shipfa.st)

**Funnel type:** Self-serve Cart Funnel with stacked OTO bundle. One page, three buy-buttons, lifetime-deal pricing, no email gate, no demo, no call. Pay-and-download.

**Hook:** "Ship your startup in days, not weeks." Sub-hook: "The NextJS boilerplate with all you need to build your SaaS, AI tool, or any other web app and make your first $ online fast."

**Story:** Marc's founder narrative is the proof. "16 startups in 2 years, now doing $45,000/month." The story is the asymmetry: a guy who could not stop shipping, who decided to sell the shovel. He doesn't tell a long origin story on the page — the numbers tell it for him.

**Offer + Stack:** Starter $199 (was $299). All-in $249 (was $349). Bundle (ShipFast + CodeFast course) $299 (was $648). Stack is dense: NextJS boilerplate, SEO/blog, Mailgun, Stripe + Lemon Squeezy, MongoDB + Supabase, Google OAuth, magic links, components, animations, ChatGPT legal prompts, Discord, leaderboard, $1,210 in partner discounts, lifetime updates.

**Guarantee / Risk Reversal:** None. Explicitly: "After you've got access to the repo, ShipFast is yours forever, so it can't be refunded." He swaps risk reversal for a counter-promise: customers "ship in 7 days on average."

**Attractive Character:** Reporter-turned-Leader. Started as a build-in-public reporter ("look what I shipped today"); now sells from earned authority. Founder = artifact.

**Polarity:** FOR speed, shipping, profit, lifetime ownership. AGAINST perfectionism, infinite tweaking, agency-grade boilerplate bloat, recurring SaaS taxes on developers.

**Social Proof Pattern:** Headline counter — "8,298 makers using ShipFast." 135k Twitter followers as authority anchor. Product Hunt "Maker of the Year 2023" badge. 25+ testimonials, most with Stripe revenue screenshots ($170 — $3,000 MRR). Proof is dollars, not adjectives.

**Pricing Page Mechanics:** Three tiers, all visible on one screen, strike-through anchoring on every price, "12 spots left at this price" urgency. No monthly toggle. No comparison table — every tier includes everything; the differences are payment provider count, course bundle, and 1:1 access.

**OTO / Upsell:** The Bundle IS the OTO collapsed into the buy-button — pre-stacked at checkout. Downstream, CodeFast course is the natural second purchase. Discord community + leaderboard create cohort gravity for repeat sales (new templates, new boilerplates, the next product).

**What I Would Swipe:**
- Live customer counter in the hook ("8,298 makers using ShipFast") — proof and FOMO in one number.
- Revenue-screenshot testimonials, not text quotes. Stripe is the most credible witness on the page.
- Strike-through anchor pricing with a finite-spots counter — converts the "I'll think about it" objection into a clock.

**What I Would Reject:**
- "No refunds, ever." Marco needs the opposite — a verifiable refund is THE objection-killer for our buyer. ShipFast can do no-refund because developers self-validate ("I can use this for anything"); Marco cannot.
- Pure speed/braggadocio Leader voice. Our Reluctant Hero won't earn the right to flex 16 startups; the swagger reads as gatekeeping to a pre-revenue founder.

## 2. Pieter Levels — Nomads.com (formerly Nomad List)

**Funnel type:** Self-serve Cart with onboarding squeeze. Pay first, then 6-step onboarding (username → birthday → bio → photo → email → access). The product IS the gate; the gate IS the funnel.

**Hook:** "🌍 Go nomad — Join a global community of remote workers living and traveling around the world." Above-fold anchor: "#1 Nomad Community since 2014."

**Story:** No about page in the classic sense. Pieter's narrative is embodied in the footer signature: "Thanks for signing up! I hope you like my site. I put a lot of effort into making it for years!" — handwritten-feeling, present-tense, one-guy-built-this. He IS the proof. 12 years of public building has done the storytelling for him.

**Offer + Stack:** Lite $9.99 (anchored from $19.98, 50% off). Full $19.99 (anchored from $39.98). One-time payments, "never again" billing. Stack includes: city data, chat (Telegram, 15k+ messages/month), dating, friend-finder, 260+ meetups/year, map.

**Guarantee / Risk Reversal:** No money-back guarantee. Risk reversal is "one-time payment, never recurring" — kills the SaaS-fatigue objection. The structural risk reversal is the price itself: $20 is below the threshold where guarantees matter.

**Attractive Character:** Adventurer. Lives the product. Travels the cities he lists. Sails. Codes in airports. Posts revenue dashboards publicly. Founder identity = customer identity.

**Polarity:** FOR independence, geographic freedom, public building, single-founder ownership, indie scale. AGAINST VC SaaS, recurring billing fatigue, corporate remote-work theater, productivity-LARPing.

**Social Proof Pattern:** Live counter — "41,571 members + 591 joined this month." Aggregated review score (9.0/5, 8,516 reviews — math is wrong on purpose, looks human). "As seen in" bar of credentialed media (NYT, FT, BBC, CNN, USA Today, CNBC, Guardian, Politico). Member testimonials cite real Twitter handles, not stock photos.

**Pricing Page Mechanics:** Two tiers stacked, both with strike-through anchors. Currency auto-detected by IP. No feature comparison — Full > Lite by implication. Stripe checkout opens inline.

**OTO / Upsell:** Sibling-product cross-sell rather than upsell. Pieter's portfolio (RemoteOK, Photo AI, Interior AI) cross-promote from each property. Lite-to-Full upgrade is the in-product OTO. The community itself is the retention engine; Telegram chat is the second product baked into the first.

**What I Would Swipe:**
- "As seen in" media bar above the fold — instantly answers "is this a real thing or a hobby project?"
- Live monthly-joiners counter ("+591 joined this month") that visibly ticks — momentum > totals.
- Human-feeling founder signature in the footer ("I put a lot of effort into this") — beats a polished About page every time for indie buyers.

**What I Would Reject:**
- One-time payment model. We're a subscription. Cargo-culting Levels here would break our $49/mo unit economics and remove the renewal moment that funds the guarantee remedy.
- Pay-before-tour squeeze. Marco is post-launch and skeptical; he needs a free diagnostic to taste the product before paying.

## 3. Arvid Kahl — The Bootstrapped Founder (thebootstrappedfounder.com)

**Funnel type:** Lead Squeeze + Authority Funnel feeding a fragmented back-end (books, course, podcast, consulting). The newsletter IS the funnel. Everything else is a back-end product the email list converts into over time.

**Hook:** "How to Start, Run, and Sell a Bootstrapped Business — by Arvid Kahl." Sub-hook on the opt-in: "Sign up for free and start learning!" — weekly Friday newsletter on bootstrapping + audience-building.

**Story:** Arvid sold FeedbackPanda, then turned the lessons into a body of work. Tone: pragmatic, calm, transparent. His own line: "I owe my entire career as a writer, podcaster, and founder to being radically transparent about my business." Story-as-strategy: he won the right to teach by selling the company first, then publishing every step of the next thing.

**Offer + Stack:** Free newsletter (lead magnet). Books: *Zero to Sold*, *The Embedded Entrepreneur* (no price displayed on homepage — friction = intentional; book pages handle the sale). Course: "Find Your Following." Consulting: Clarity Call (1:1) + Twitter Teardown service. Podcast: free, with sponsorships as the real product.

**Guarantee / Risk Reversal:** None visible on the homepage. The risk reversal IS the free newsletter — give an email, sample the voice, then decide. The guarantee on the back-end products is implicit reputational ("Arvid wouldn't ship junk").

**Attractive Character:** Reporter / Reluctant Hero hybrid. The "calm businessperson" identity. He's NOT selling hustle. He's selling sustainable. Founder identity = bookworm who built a real exit.

**Polarity:** FOR sustainable bootstrapping, calm businesses, transparency, building in public, audience-first. AGAINST VC hype, growth-at-all-costs, hustle culture, opaque founder PR.

**Social Proof Pattern:** Minimal on the homepage — no subscriber counter, no testimonial wall. Authority is asserted by author photo + book covers + podcast logos. The proof is the body of work itself. (This is a deliberate choice; an audience-first brand doesn't need to broadcast numbers.)

**Pricing Page Mechanics:** No central pricing page. Each product (book, course, consulting) has its own destination. Monetization is intentionally spread — newsletter → multiple back-end SKUs, never a single funnel.

**OTO / Upsell:** Newsletter is the front door; everything else is a back-end ladder — book → course → Clarity Call → Twitter Teardown. The podcast doubles as a top-of-funnel feeder AND a back-end engagement engine. Consulting is the highest-margin SKU, gated by reputation.

**What I Would Swipe:**
- Newsletter-as-front-door with a single clear weekly delivery ("every Friday"). It removes the "what do I get and how often" friction.
- Founder identity as the brand spine — Arvid IS the product even when he's selling a book. UnlockSaaS needs Maryan named on the page the same way.
- Multiple back-end SKUs fed by one list. We don't need to launch them day-one, but the architecture (front-end → list → ladder) is the right shape.

**What I Would Reject:**
- No homepage social proof / no counters. Arvid earned the right to be quiet with numbers; we haven't. We need visible proof early.
- Fragmented monetization with no clear single hero offer up front. Marco needs ONE buy-now button on the front page, not a menu.

## 4. Marc Köhlbrugge — WIP (wip.co)

**Funnel type:** Application / Curated Membership Funnel. Closed community, invite-gated, subscription-based. Squeeze is social: you don't apply, you get invited.

**Hook:** "We're a group of makers shipping together. We help each other stay accountable and reach our goals." Sub-hook: "Join the maker community."

**Story:** Marc Köhlbrugge built BetaList first, then WIP as a maker-accountability tool for himself, then opened it. The story is muted on the page — like Nomads.com, the proof is the visible roster (Pieter Levels, Andrey Azimov, jasonleow, Jeff Triplett are on the homepage avatars). The community is the story.

**Offer + Stack:** Pricing not on the public page (gated by checkout flow, Stripe integration). What members get: daily progress sharing (todo streaks), peer learning, project feedback, bi-weekly video calls, member-only deals. The streak/leaderboard mechanic IS half the product.

**Guarantee / Risk Reversal:** None visible. Risk reversal is the curation itself — "Members are invited by existing members." Implicit promise: you'll be among serious shippers, not tire-kickers.

**Attractive Character:** Reluctant Leader. Marc rarely centers himself; the community avatars do the talking. Founder-as-host, not founder-as-guru. Closer to Reporter than Adventurer.

**Polarity:** FOR shipping daily, public accountability, peer pressure, curated quality, small focused groups. AGAINST broadcast-community noise, low-signal Discord servers, "anyone can join" forums, vanity members.

**Social Proof Pattern:** 3,702 members counter. Nine real maker avatars on the homepage (Pieter Levels among them — borrowed authority). Curation badge — "invited by existing members." No testimonials needed; the avatar wall does the work.

**Pricing Page Mechanics:** Hidden until "Join now" click. Subscription model. No tiered comparison. The friction is the message: "if you have to ask the price, you might not be the right member."

**OTO / Upsell:** Member-only deals are the in-funnel cross-sells (partner discounts). Sibling products (BetaList) cross-promote. The bi-weekly video calls are the engagement/retention OTO.

**What I Would Swipe:**
- Avatar wall of real, recognizable users on the homepage. Worth more than ten testimonials. Borrowed authority compounds.
- Streak mechanic / daily todo as the product's central ritual — the same DOING-environment we already locked in our Step 5.
- Curation as risk reversal. "Curated" reframes price objection into status acquisition.

**What I Would Reject:**
- Invite-only gating. Our funnel needs to convert cold Marco, who has zero indie-hacker network. Curation would suffocate our top of funnel.
- Hidden pricing. Marco is a skeptic. Hiding price reads as "I can't defend my price" — exactly the dollar-objection we documented.

## 5. Marc Lou — IndiePage (indiepa.ge)

**Funnel type:** Free-tier-to-paid SaaS with social-proof-as-subheadline. Different funnel shape from ShipFast even though same founder. Worth hacking separately because the architecture is closer to ours.

**Hook:** "Showcase and grow your startups." Sub-headline carries the weight: "21,807 Solopreneurs are already remarkable!" — a count masquerading as a sub-hook.

**Story:** Marc's story is implied via portfolio link and the indie-hacker tone ("banger," emoji-laden voice). The page itself does no origin storytelling — the 21k count is the story.

**Offer + Stack:** Free profile page. Paid tier unlocks custom domain, analytics, leaderboard position. Pricing model: "One-time payment. No subscription."

**Guarantee / Risk Reversal:** None. The free tier IS the risk reversal — claim a page free, decide later if you want to pay.

**Attractive Character:** Indie peer — first-name basis with the audience. Endorsements from Damon Chen, Arvid Kahl displayed prominently. Founder-as-member-of-the-tribe.

**Polarity:** FOR public building, indie identity, one-time pricing, portfolio shipping. AGAINST subscription fatigue, faceless founders, anonymous bio pages.

**Social Proof Pattern:** Subheadline IS the proof — "21,807 Solopreneurs." Grid of 24+ named founder profile cards (faces + their startup counts ranging 1 to 35). Peer endorsements with profile photos.

**Pricing Page Mechanics:** Free tier prominent. Paid tier reveals at the upgrade prompt. No long-form sales page — conversion happens after attachment to a personal profile.

**OTO / Upsell:** Custom domain + analytics + leaderboard are the natural upsells. ShipFast cross-promotes from IndiePage's founder bar.

**What I Would Swipe:**
- **Count-as-subheadline.** When N ≥ 100, replace funnel hub sub with "Joined by N Verified Builders" — workbook 04 §2.
- **Founder-card wall.** OG image route at `/builder/[slug]/opengraph-image.tsx` is already shipped. Add a `/builders` index that renders the wall once N ≥ 20 — workbook 10 butterfly marketing loop 2 already specs this.
- **Free-tier-as-front-door.** The Free Diagnostic IS our equivalent. Make the diagnosis result claimable as a `/builder/[your-slug]` page (free, persistent). This turns the diagnostic into a viral lead-magnet AND seeds the founder-card wall.

**What I Would Reject:**
- **One-time pricing for the core.** Our subscription is load-bearing for the guarantee economics. The $1 Starter already covers the "one-time entry point" itch.
- **Vanity monetization** (custom domains, leaderboards). Marco's pain is revenue, not vanity. The Verified Builder badge must always tie to a Stripe-verified result, never to "I bought The Machine."

## 6. Justin Welsh — justinwelsh.me

**Funnel type:** Lead-magnet-to-newsletter-to-cohort with media-logo authority. Heaviest social proof via media credentials, lightest via testimonials.

**Hook:** "It's time to rethink how you work, earn, and live." Abstract identity-led hook — sells the worldview before the product.

**Offer + Stack:** Free lead magnet: "110 Revenue-Generating Content Ideas" PDF. Free newsletter: Saturday Solopreneur (175,000+ subscribers). Products listed as "Coming Soon": Creator MBA Masterclass, LinkedIn OS, Content OS. (Historically $150 each when live.)

**Guarantee / Risk Reversal:** None visible. The 175k newsletter count IS the implicit risk reversal — "if 175k people stay subscribed, I am safe to try."

**Attractive Character:** Reporter / quiet expert. Doesn't broadcast wealth or hustle. Calm, professional. Founder-as-mentor.

**Polarity:** FOR solopreneurship, freedom-focused entrepreneurship, sustainable income, async work. AGAINST chasing maximum profits, growth-at-all-costs, hustle culture.

**Social Proof Pattern:** Media-logo bar (Forbes / BI / Entrepreneur / Vice / IH). Singular subscriber count (175,000+). NO customer testimonials visible. Trust line: "I will never spam or sell your info. Ever."

**Pricing Page Mechanics:** No prices visible. Products in "Coming Soon" pipeline. Newsletter is the only live conversion.

**OTO / Upsell:** Newsletter → product launch → cohort. Long lead time between subscribe and offer.

- **Named, specific lead magnet PDF.** "110 ideas" is more conversion-active than "subscribe to my newsletter." - **"I will never spam" trust line.** Add below the diagnostic-form email field: "Your email enters a 5-day sequence and a weekly Friday note. Unsubscribe wipes you in 1 click. I never sell your data." Workbook 04 §5 covers the sequence; we just don't say so on the squeeze.
- **Worldview hook above product.** "It's time to rethink how you work, earn, and live" sells the philosophy first. Our equivalent: the polarity AGAINST sentence on the homepage hero. Already shipped — confirmed correct.

**What I Would Reject:**
- **Coming-Soon product status.** Justin can do this because his 175k newsletter forgives the delay. We have 0 subscribers — every promised product must ship within the sprint or get cut.
- **Media logos for credibility.** We have none. Don't fake it. Replace with "Verified Builders shipped from" once we have customer logos.
- **Worldview-only positioning.** Justin sells the philosophy; he doesn't enforce the result. Our differentiation is that the Machine doesn't let Marco out without the result OR the refund. Reinforce that on every CTA.

## 7. Daniel Vassallo — Small Bets (smallbets.com)

**Funnel type:** Lifetime-deal community on a single sales page. Closest model in this set for our future "Rung 2" ascension tier.

**Hook:** "53 Expert-Led Classes. One Payment. Yours Forever." Specific count + lifetime framing anchors value before price.

**Sub:** "Instant access to our full library of recorded classes from top practitioners plus a vibrant community of 7,712 members."

**Story:** Daniel doesn't open with autobiography on the sales page. He appears as one of multiple instructors. The community of 7,712 is the story; the 53 classes are the proof.

**Offer + Stack:** 53 recorded expert-led classes + access to 7,712-member community + "Feedback on your projects in our Campfire chat" + lifetime access to all content. Price ~$300 one-time (you scroll to find it).

**Guarantee / Risk Reversal:** **"100% satisfaction or your money back. No questions asked."** Unconditional. The most aligned guarantee posture to ours in this study.

**Attractive Character:** Pragmatic counter-hustle leader. Sells portfolio-of-bets thinking instead of all-in-on-one-startup gospel. Founder-as-thinker.

**Polarity:** FOR small bets, portfolio income, solopreneur risk-spreading, lifetime ownership. AGAINST VC scaling, all-in-on-one-bet thinking, hustle culture, recurring subscriptions.

**Social Proof Pattern:** 7,712 member count, prominent. **35+ written testimonials** — including "Worth 10x the cost" and "Watching 6 hours of Small Bets Fundamentals has done me more good than 3+ years of striving." NO video testimonials, NO MRR screenshots. Text quotes at high volume do the work.

**Pricing Page Mechanics:** Price below fold (~$300 one-time). Single long sales page. **No drip sequence, no upsell, no OTO.** Conversion happens on-page or not at all.

**Critical — Validates Our Decision:** **NO urgency, NO scarcity, NO countdown timers.** Same call we made in workbook 07. Validates the discipline — Daniel's audience (Marco-adjacent) also rejects fake scarcity. We are in good company on this rejection.

**OTO / Upsell:** None. Single deliverable. The community itself is the retention mechanism.

**What I Would Swipe:**
- **Member count as anchor.** Mirror IndiePage swipe — when N ≥ 100, add "Joined by N Verified Builders" to hero.
- **35+ testimonial wall.** Workbook 06 already has the rewrites; what's missing is the testimonial-capture flow. Add: each Verified Builder + 1 Verified Customer pair gets a testimonial slot. Format: 1-sentence headline + 2-sentence body + name + product link. Target 10 by month 3, 30 by month 6.
- **"Worth 10x the cost" framing.** Coach the ask: testimonials should explicitly reference dollar math. "$49 to get my first $X customer was the easiest math I've ever done."
- **Unconditional satisfaction guarantee posture, deliberately adapted.** Daniel's is unconditional. Ours is conditional on in-product milestones. Document the WHY on the FAQ: "Your refund is automatic if Stripe shows no new charge AND you completed the in-product work. No human in the loop." Sharpen the mechanic; do not weaken to unconditional (the condition is what makes the economics survive low conversion).
- **Validation of our scarcity-rejection.** Confirms the workbook 07 call. Cross-link in the workbook: "Small Bets ($300, 7k+ members, no scarcity) validates the discipline."

**What I Would Reject:**
- **One-time pricing for the core.** Same logic as IndiePage. Subscription is our economic backbone.
- **Breadth-over-depth (53 classes).** Daniel sells breadth. We sell ONE outcome. Stack discipline — single result. Don't drift toward "and 12 other things you'll get."

## 8. Anti-Hack — Pieter Levels personal site (levels.io)

**Studied so we deliberately do NOT model it.**

**What it is:** Single-page identity broadcast. "A few years ago I sold all my stuff to explore the world." Subscribe button. Twitter link. Project list. No funnel, no offer, no guarantee, no sequence.

**Why it works for Pieter:**
- 500k+ Twitter following — distribution does what the funnel would do
- Consumer products (Photo AI, RemoteOK, Nomads.com) — buyers find him via Twitter/Product Hunt, not via his personal page
- $1M+/yr revenue claim — earned through 10+ years of public building
- His personal page is a hub, not a conversion surface. The conversion happens on Nomads.com (already hacked in section 2)

**Why it would kill UnlockSaaS:**
- We have 0 → growing following
- Skeptic Marco-avatar needs more than identity to trust an offer
- Our product requires belief work the homepage MUST do — workbook 06 four core stories all need surface area
- Pieter's funnel works *because of* the distribution he already has; we don't have it

**The temptation we're refusing:** "Just ship a single-page brand and tweet from there." This is exactly Marco's avoidance pattern named in workbook 01 §6 Beat 1 Flaw 4 ("Plans Before Shipping" — defaulting to thinking-about-doing instead of doing) generalized to marketing. Anti-hack reinforces workbook 09 §1 launch-minimum channel discipline.

**Conclusion:** the funnel hub at `/` must do conversion work, not just identity broadcast. Confirmed correct. Hack closed.

## 9. Reddit / Indie Hackers — Converting-Thread Structure Hack (v3, public-source)

**Why this section now exists:** v2 acknowledged "No Reddit/IH thread hacks — Workbook 08 dream-100.csv lists the forums; mining converting threads on r/SaaS, r/microsaas, IH is a separate exercise (Hack v3 candidate)." It is now done. The source is the same body of public threads already documented in `strategy/dollar-objections.md` — 6 IH/HN threads with 30+ named-user verbatim quotes — re-read through a different lens: **not "what objections do they raise" but "what STRUCTURE makes this thread convert (upvotes, replies, follow-up DMs, profile-bio clicks)?"** Hacking structure, not language.

**Six threads hacked** (overlap with dollar-objections.md is intentional — same source, different question):

| # | Thread shape | Source | Why it converts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I built X products in Y years and made $Z" — autobiographical timeline | IH "37 products in 5 years" (AlexBelogubov), IH "3 years to $5k MRR + burnout" | Founder-as-evidence. No teaching, no advice. The story IS the credential. Comments compound when the timeline is honest about failures. |
| 2 | "Subscriptions vs one-time" — open category question | IH "Subscriptions vs One-Time" thread (Smoteria, Bpve, ddio, GeoNite, WASOS) | Asks a polarizing structural question, NOT "what should I do." Audience answers each other; OP harvests language. |
| 3 | "Which SaaS do you actually pay for" — implicit social-proof aggregation | IH "Which SaaS do you pay for" (Cleme, lydia_stepanek) | Survey-as-content. Replies become a curated SaaS list. OP becomes the curator of the list. |
| 4 | "Here is the dumbest mistake I made" — confession-with-lesson | IH "blindly followed a lot of advice from 'gurus'" thread | Anti-guru polarity. The community amplifies confession because it validates their own anti-guru priors. |
| 5 | "Show HN: I built [specific narrow thing]" — single-feature, honest scope | HN Show posts in dollar-objections category | Specificity earns clicks. "AI tool" gets ignored; "an open-source SQLite GUI for vim users" gets the front page. |
| 6 | "I'm shutting down [product]" — post-mortem with revenue numbers | IH shutdown threads referenced in dollar-objections.md Category 3 | Vulnerability + numbers + lessons = highest engagement-per-word on IH. The honest post-mortem gets cited for years. |

### What I would swipe

- **Shape #1 (autobiographical timeline)** for `/about` and IH posts. Maryan's flat-Stripe-line story IS this shape. Workbook 01 §4 has the 5-beat Epiphany Bridge already drafted — when posted as a thread on IH, it would be one example of Shape #1.
- **Shape #2 (open category question)** for week-2 IH post. Suggested title: "Founders who shipped with AI tools and hit $0/mo — what did you do next?" This is the dollar-objections.md harvest mechanism extended into a public publishing motion. Replies become next month's content.
- **Shape #4 (confession-with-lesson)** is the Parable 3 (SEO Escape Hatch) post. Already in `strategy/content-queue-week-1.md`. Cross-verified: matches the converting structure.

## 10. Newsletter Sequence Hack (v3, public-archive pass)

**Why this section now exists:** v2 acknowledged "No email-sequence hacks. Did not subscribe to each newsletter and reverse-engineer the welcome sequence. Defer to Sprint 4 retrospective." The actual deferral was wrong: most of these sequences have public web archives that can be mined without subscribing. Buffer's old welcome sequence, Arvid Kahl's first-3 issues, Justin Welsh's lead-magnet delivery, Lenny's Friday-cadence subject patterns — all are visible without an inbox commitment.

**Four sequence patterns mined from public archives:**

### Pattern A — Arvid Kahl, weekly Friday (The Bootstrapped Founder)

- **Named day, named time.** "Every Friday" creates an anchor the subscriber can predict.
- **Subject pattern:** `[Topic clause] — [issue number]`. Example: "Building a transparent founder brand — Issue 167."
- **Open with a personal note.** Two-sentence preface in first person: "I've been thinking about X this week..." Then the essay.
- **Single CTA at the foot.** Reply-to or book link. Never multi-link.
- **Cadence discipline:** zero promotional pushes between issues. The Friday slot is sacred; everything else channels through the back-end products listed on his homepage (book, course, consulting).

**Swipe for Seinfeld:** name the day (Tuesday, per Cookbook Swipe 5), open with a 2-sentence first-person preface, single CTA at the foot. Workbook 09 §6 already has this; v3 confirms it from a converted public archive.

### Pattern B — Justin Welsh, Saturday Solopreneur (175k subs)

- **Single-screen issues.** ~300 words. Subscriber can finish in 90 seconds.
- **Three sections per issue:** Hook (one paragraph), Story (one paragraph), Tactic (one numbered list of 3–5 items).
- **Pre-named lead magnet delivery:** "110 Revenue-Generating Content Ideas" PDF on opt-in. The number IS the conversion driver — "subscribe to my newsletter" converts at 3-5%; "get the 110 ideas" converts at 12-18% per Justin's own posted analytics.
- **NO long-form essays.** Discipline: every issue fits on a phone screen.

**Swipe for Soap Opera Email 5:** named lead-magnet alternative path (closes Cookbook Swipe 9 — "10 Founders, 10 Flat Stripe Lines: What Killed Them and What Worked", a 6-page PDF derived from `strategy/dollar-objections.md`). Subscribers who skip the diagnostic get the PDF; subscribers who completed the diagnostic get the Day-0 personalized label. Two doors into the same Soap Opera.

### Pattern C — Indie Hackers weekly digest

- **Curated digest, not editorial.** Each issue = 5 community thread headlines + 5 milestone announcements + 1 sponsor.
- **Reply mechanism:** every issue ends with "Reply with the question you wanted to ask this week — I read every one." Creates a manual signal for IH editors.
- **Sponsor placement:** single sponsor per issue, native-styled, never display-banner.

**Swipe for the Founding Cohort follow-up:** when the first cohort lands, the weekly "Verified Builder digest" inherits this exact shape — 5 builder updates + 1 founder milestone + 1 sponsor (eventually a partner from `strategy/dream-100-outreach.md` §4). Defer build until 10 paying founders exist.

### Pattern D — ShipFast launch sequence (Marc Lou, reconstructed from public X posts)

- **Pre-launch:** 4 weeks of build-in-public X threads with the SAME hook ("Ship your startup in days, not weeks"). Repetition compounds recognition.
- **Launch day:** single X thread linking the page. No email blast (no list at launch). The Twitter following IS the list.
- **Post-launch:** weekly "16 startups in 2 years, here's how I shipped this week's one" thread. Every thread reinforces the founder credential.

**Swipe (DEFERRED with rationale):** Marc has 135k X followers; UnlockSaaS has zero list and ~few hundred follower starting point. The pre-launch shape works when distribution exists. Document the pattern; do NOT cargo-cult the cadence at launch — workbook 09 §1 already has the right launch cadence for our distribution reality (1 thread/week + 5 DMs/week to Tier A).

### What this section ships into the workbooks

- **Workbook 09 §6 (Soap Opera + Seinfeld):** confirms Tuesday for Seinfeld weekly anchor (Pattern A); confirms single-CTA discipline (Pattern A); confirms ~300-word issue length cap when on Seinfeld cadence (Pattern B).
- **Workbook 04 §3 Diagnostic alternative path:** the 10-Founders PDF lead-magnet (Pattern B) earns its own row in the action matrix (Row 14 in v2; status updated below).
- **Workbook 10 §5 (Butterfly Marketing):** weekly Verified Builder digest enters the play list, gated to 10+ paying founders (Pattern C).
