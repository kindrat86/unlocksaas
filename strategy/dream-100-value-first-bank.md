## §0 — Why a reply bank (and what makes a reply work)

`dream-100-outreach.md` §1 prescribes "1 substantive public reply on their newest post" per Tier-A target, twice a week. The dossier in §2 had ONE DM per target and ZERO ready-to-deploy public-reply text. The result: every Tuesday and Wednesday during the warm-up, the founder faces a blank reply box and decision fatigue.

This bank closes that gap. Per target, three reply shapes are pre-written, anchored to that target's specific content patterns. The founder picks the shape that matches the target's most recent post and fills in the one specific reference from the post.

### What makes a public reply work (Brunson reply rules — applied to X / IH / Reddit)

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The first 14 characters must contain a specific reference to the target's post.** | If the reply could be cut and pasted onto any of their posts, it dies. |
| 2 | **Contains an insight, not a compliment.** | Compliments without insight read as fishing. The reply must add value to the thread. |
| 3 | **Ends with a question.** | Questions get replies; statements get likes. Replies build relationships; likes don't. |
| 4 | **Zero links.** | The first message containing a link IS the pitch. Public-reply phase is pre-pitch. |
| 5 | **Mentions a specific data point or experience.** | "I saw the same thing in 10+ founder conversations" beats "I agree." |
| 6 | **Under 280 characters on X; 1–3 paragraphs on IH; 3–5 sentences on Reddit.** | Each platform's reply-readability ceiling. |
| 7 | **NEVER mentions UnlockSaaS, The Machine, or any link to our site.** | Mentions are reserved for the response-after-the-target-DMs-back. |
| 8 | **Always signs with first name or initials only on long-form (IH); never signs on short-form (X).** | X signature is a tell of LinkedIn-thinking; absent on indie / startup X. |

### How to use this bank

1. Open the dossier in `dream-100-outreach.md` §2 for the target whose week it is.
2. Open this file at the same target's section.
3. Read their most recent post.
4. Pick the shape (A, B, or C) that matches their post type.
5. Fill the `[bracketed]` slot with one specific reference from the post.
6. Post.
7. Log the touch in `dream_100_touches` (via `scripts/log-touch.py` once shipped, or manually for now).

## §5 — IH long-form replies (different shape)

IH replies are not 280 characters — they're 1–3 paragraphs. Pattern:
- Paragraph 1: specific reference to the original post + the insight from your 10+ founder conversations
- Paragraph 2 (optional): one piece of contrarian data
- Paragraph 3 (required): one question to the original poster

Three IH-shaped templates:

### IH shape A — when the original post is a launch / revenue story
> Read this carefully because [specific element from their post] is the part I've been wrestling with personally. Background: I'm a non-engineer who shipped a dozen AI products with Lovable and Claude, watched all of them flatline in Stripe, and ran a year of denial-coded SEO before realizing the bottleneck was upstream of the build.
>
> What [specific element] surfaces for me is that the post-launch quiet death looks identical to the survivor story until about week 8 or 12 — and the difference isn't the product, it's whether the founder ever wrote one specific person's name on the offer page.
>
> Question for you: at what point in your launch did you know whether it was going to convert or quietly die — and what was the first signal that told you?

### IH shape B — when the original post is a "lessons learned" essay
> [Lesson #N from their post] is the one I'd want to underline twice. The 10+ post-launch pre-revenue founders I sat with this year all had this exact pattern: praise-without-payment, then attribution to the product, then a SEO / launch-tactic detour, then quiet.
>
> Contrarian data point from those conversations: every founder who broke out of the loop did the same single move — they had one conversation with one named human about the specific result the product delivered, then wrote that result on the offer page.
>
> What's the move you took that you'd retroactively name the inflection point?

### IH shape C — when the original post is a question to the community
> Direct answer first: [your specific answer in one sentence].
>
> The 10+ founders I sat with this year on this exact question said roughly: [aggregate pattern]. The pattern that surprised me: [specific contrarian observation].
>
> Curious if anyone else seeing [the surprising thing] — or am I cherrypicking?

## §6 — Reddit replies (third shape)

Reddit kills overt promotion harder than X or IH. Reddit replies must be 3–5 sentences, value-first, no link, no signature.

### Reddit shape A — answer the question, briefly
> Direct answer: [specific answer]. Context: I've talked to 10+ post-launch pre-revenue founders this year on the same shape, and the pattern that broke them out wasn't [common assumption], it was [contrarian observation]. The thing I'd add on [OP's specific situation]: [one practical action].

### Reddit shape B — gentle contrarian
> Gentle pushback on [specific thing in OP's post]. From the 10+ founder conversations I had this year, the people who actually got to first paying customer didn't [common move], they did [contrarian move]. Not saying it's wrong, but the alternative is rarely considered.

### Reddit shape C — share a specific data point
> The number on this is genuinely useful: I tracked across 10+ post-launch pre-revenue founders this year, and the median time from launch to first paying customer was [data point]. The biggest delta wasn't [obvious thing], it was [specific thing]. Maybe useful for your decision.

## §8 — What this bank does NOT do

1. **Post automatically.** Maryan posts from his real accounts. The bank is decision-fatigue removal, not auto-posting.
2. **Replace the DMs.** DMs are in `dream-100-outreach.md` §2. Replies (public) are step 1; DMs (private) are step 3.
3. **Cover all Dream 100 categories.** Only Category 2 (influencers / individuals, n=20). Categories 1 (communities), 3 (podcasts), 4 (newsletters), 5 (partner SaaS), 6 (YouTube), 7 (blogs) have their own engagement patterns elsewhere.
4. **Stay static.** As targets ship new content shapes, new template variants get added. Maintain quarterly.
