## Script 8: The Founding-Cohort Product Launch Sequence — REVISED FROM SKIPPED

**Revision date:** 2026-05-17. Earlier version of this workbook skipped Brunson's Product Launch Funnel (Jeff Walker style) with the reasoning that an evergreen SaaS subscription has no cart-open event. That reasoning was wrong for exactly one moment in this product's lifetime: the transition from zero-traffic to in-market. There IS a discrete launch event — the founding cohort. After that the funnel reverts to evergreen, and the Soap Opera Sequence carries the day-to-day load. The PLF runs **once**.

### Why PLF fits the founding-cohort moment

Three convergences make the founding cohort the right place for a Brunson Product Launch:

1. **Real scarcity, not fake.** The cap is 50 seats — the same number that workbook 10 names as the Phase 3 trigger. A Verified Builder badge variant ("Founding Verified Builder") is mathematically distinct because there is only one cohort of 50, ever. The skeptic avatar tolerates this because the scarcity is structural, not a countdown timer that resets on refresh.
2. **The price stays $49/mo.** No discount. No "founding members get 50% off." The bonus is a **lifetime price lock at $49** (defensible because future evergreen pricing will eventually rise) plus the founding badge plus 30 days of direct-line access to Maryan. Brunson rule: never train them to wait for a sale.
3. **The Phase 3 trigger collapses into a single launch event.** Workbook 10's growth map said "50 paying customers" was the Phase 3 unlock. The Founding Cohort PLF makes that event a deliberate motion instead of a 6–12 month organic accretion. The customer-acquisition velocity question changes from "how do we get to 50" to "how do we fill the cart in 7 days."

### Cohort cap mechanics

- **Cap:** 50 seats. Real. Enforced by Stripe webhook reading `founding_cohort` row count before granting the founding bonus on `customer.subscription.created`.
- **Window:** 7 days from cart open OR 50 seats, whichever closes first.
- **Post-window behaviour:** the founding bonuses (lifetime price lock, founding badge, direct line) are retired forever. The product itself is still on sale at the same $49/mo. The cap is the BONUS gate, not the PRODUCT gate.
- **No discount.** Same $49. The math: 50 seats × $49 × 12 months = $29,400 ARR baseline if zero churn. With Brunson SaaS-realistic 5% monthly churn, contribution is ~$22k Year 1.

### The launch page (`/founding`)

Single page. Same Reluctant Hero voice. Components, top to bottom:

1. **Hero** — "50 Founding Verified Builders. 7-day window. Lifetime price lock."
2. **Cohort meter** — server-rendered "X of 50 seats claimed" with a thin progress bar. Updates on every page load (no fake live counter).
3. **The three PLVs** — embedded one after the other, ungated. By the time someone is on `/founding` cold, they have already watched at least PLV3 in the email sequence; the embeds are for replay + share.
4. **The stack** — same stack as the $49 sales page (workbook 07 Section 3) PLUS the three founding bonuses listed below.
5. **The guarantee** — same 60-day Stripe-verified guarantee. Restated verbatim.
6. **The cart-open CTA** — single button: "Claim seat #[next_available] for $49/mo." Disabled with copy "50 of 50 claimed. The door is closed." when cap is reached.
7. **The cart-close timer** — real, server-rendered, ticks down to cart-close moment OR cap. Not a fake reset-on-refresh counter.
8. **AGAINST line** — same enemy sentence from the homepage.

### The three founding bonuses (real, defensible)

| Bonus | What it is | Cost to deliver | Why a skeptic accepts it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lifetime $49/mo price lock | Maryan commits in writing: your $49/mo never goes up, for the life of your active subscription. | Zero out of pocket. Forfeit of future-price-increase revenue. Acceptable because the price WILL rise to $79/mo by Phase 3 anyway, so the lock is real. | The skeptic can math this. $30/mo difference × 24 months = $720 of locked-in value. Defensible without inflation. |
| Founding Verified Builder badge | Distinct variant of the existing Verified Builder OG image. Different visual frame. Public on `/builder/[slug]`. | Zero — extends the existing `builder_badges` taxonomy. | Identity-anchored social proof. The skeptic gets in early on a movement that will have history. |
| 30-day direct line | First 30 days: Maryan replies personally to any email at maryan@unlocksaas.com within 1 business day. | Maryan's time (capped at 50 founders × 30 days; manageable for one founder). | The skeptic gets the founder's brain, not a help-desk macro. Removes "what if I get stuck" objection. |

**Total founding bonus value (math, not fantasy):** ~$720 price lock + ~$0 badge + ~$200 direct-line equivalent = ~$920 of defensible additional value on a $49/mo subscription. Brings the founding-cohort offer to ~$1,416 total value / $49 price = 28.9x. Still defensible because the price lock is the bulk of it and that math is provable.
