## Section 2: Free Diagnostic Lead Funnel (Rung 0)

**Purpose:** Anonymous traffic to known email. Doubles as the SEO / AEO bait, because a diagnostic that returns a real labeled answer is a sharable artifact.

### Pages

1. **Squeeze page.** Hook on top, two-field form (email plus product URL), AC three-line about beneath. CTA: "See why your launch is flat."
2. **Diagnostic result page.** A live read of the submitted URL returns one of three labels: **Wrong Person**, **Weak Offer**, or **Weak Belief**. Each label has a 100-word Reluctant Hero explanation and a single CTA to the $1 Starter.
3. **Nurture sequence.** 5-email Soap Opera over 5 days (full copy in workbook 04, Section 5).

### Hook / Story / Offer for this funnel

- **Hook:** Hook #3 from Section 5 (the pain mirror) on the squeeze. Hook #10 (the contrarian) for cold ads pointing in.
- **Story:** The three-line about opener (Section 6, Beat 2).
- **Offer:** The diagnosis itself. Framing: "Get the same diagnosis we charge $49/mo to fix, for free."

### The 7-Phase Customer Journey (per DotCom Secrets)

| Phase | What it does | Where in the funnel |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Pre-frame bridge | Hook, parable, polarity carry the cold reader in | Cold ad, post, podcast guest open |
| 2. Qualify subscribers | They give email to see the diagnosis | Squeeze form |
| 3. Qualify buyers | Some click to the $1 Starter | Result-page CTA |
| 4. Identify hyperactive buyers | Some take the $49 OTO from $1 immediately | OTO inside the $1 funnel |
| 5. Age plus ascend | Nurture sequence does the work for the cold ones | 5-email Soap Opera |
| 6. Change selling environment | Cold to warm to paying | Free, then $1, then $49 |
| 7. Pull a new opportunity | Polarity FOR / AGAINST + enemy line | Every page |

## Section 3: $1 Starter Unboxing Funnel (Rung 1)

**Purpose:** Email to first-time buyer. Marco does Machine Steps 1 and 2 (dream customer + offer) and walks out with a real finished WHO and WHAT.

### Pages

1. **Sales page.** Hook + Star Story Solution + offer + 10x value framing + $1 CTA.
2. **Order form.** Stripe one-time checkout. Single page.
3. **OTO.** One question, two buttons: upgrade to $49/mo Machine plus the 60-day guarantee, or continue with just the Starter.
4. **Confirmation plus member area.** Direct entry to Machine Step 1.

### Hook / Story / Offer

- **Hook:** "Finish your dream customer and your offer this week. For one dollar."
- **Story:** Section 6 three-line about opener as the sub-headline. Star Story Solution block (workbook 03, Script 3) as the page body.
- **Offer:** $1 one-time. A complete small win: finished WHO and finished WHAT. Stops short of a paying customer (that result stays inside the $49 core).

### Design rule

The Starter delivers Machine Steps 1 and 2 only. It does NOT give away Steps 3 to 7. Restraint is the design discipline. A fragment of a result insults a skeptic. A complete small result earns the next dollar.

## Section 4: $49/mo Machine Presentation Funnel (Rung 2)

## Section 6: One Offer, One Funnel (Secret #26)

The Brunson rule. One funnel at a time. For Unlock SaaS:

**The first funnel is the $1 Starter Unboxing Funnel.**

Five reasons:
1. Cheapest, fastest test of the actual offer copy and the Star Story Solution.
2. Identifies real buyers. A $1 charge is the smallest possible Marco-Meter.
3. Pre-qualifies ascent to $49 via the OTO.
4. The Free Diagnostic is downstream-simple to build (squeeze + tool + emails), so it slots in second.
5. The $49 Presentation funnel reuses about 90% of the $1 funnel's copy at longer form, so it is third and cheapest to build last.

### The build order is locked

1. $1 Starter Unboxing Funnel (full end-to-end, Stripe live, OTO live).
2. Free Diagnostic Lead Funnel (squeeze + result page + 5-email Soap Opera).
3. $49 Machine Presentation Funnel (full sales page + onboarding + 7 Machine steps).

Do not parallelize. Ship the $1 funnel end-to-end before touching the next.
