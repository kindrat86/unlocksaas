## Section 4: Secret #19, Cold Traffic (Eugene Schwartz Awareness)

### Awareness Levels (mapped to Marco)

| Awareness | Description | Marco's version | Copy approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Doesn't know problem exists | New founder pre-launch | DO NOT TARGET. Wrong avatar. |
| Problem-aware | Knows there is a problem | Founder with flat Stripe; blames product | "Your launch is flat. Here is what is actually causing it." (curiosity hook) |
| Solution-aware | Knows there are solutions | Has tried tactics; still flat | "You tried more traffic. Here is the work nobody told you to do." (contrarian hook) |
| Product-aware | Knows Unlock SaaS exists | Read the Free Diagnostic | "60 days. Verified by your own Stripe. Or you pay nothing." (offer hook) |
| Most-aware | Buys related products | Pays for IH Pro, etc. | "You are one funnel away. Run The Machine." (direct hook) |

### Cold-Traffic Avatar

Different from sales avatar. Cold Marco is problem-aware. Has not read your story. Clicks on hooks that mirror his pain, not hooks that promise results.

Cold-traffic hook priority (from workbook 01 Section 5):
1. Hook #3 (pain mirror): highest cold-traffic CTR.
2. Hook #10 (contrarian reframe): second.
3. Hook #1 (curiosity, soft): third.
Hook #8 (guarantee) goes on RETARGETING, not cold (premature for problem-aware).

### Cold-traffic bridges

A cold visitor cannot land directly on the $49 sales page. Bridges:

```
Cold ad
  ↓
Bridge page (short pre-sell: parable + diagnostic CTA)
  ↓
Free Diagnostic squeeze
  ↓
Diagnostic result page
  ↓
$1 Starter or 5-email Soap Opera
```

NEVER cold to $49. Always cold to free to $1 to $49.

## Section 5: Secret #20, Other Growth Hacks

### Butterfly Marketing (small viral loops)

Three plays inside The Machine:

| Play | What it does | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Shareable diagnostic result | Diagnostic page is shareable (with permission). Each share adds a "diagnose your launch" CTA. | End of diagnostic |
| 2. Public proof badge | First Paying Customer Verified fires a shareable badge for X / LinkedIn ("Verified Builder, first paying customer at [product]") | First-paying-customer event |
| 3. Affiliate amplification | When a customer shares their badge, one-click "earn 30% recurring for 6 months for anyone who joins via your badge" appears | Immediately after badge share |

### Other tactical growth hacks (deferred but noted)

- SEO programmatic pages ("first paying customer for [niche]" template, generated from Machine outputs)
- Free Diagnostic widget for partner SaaS to embed
- Public weekly metrics dashboard (founder's own progress, build-in-public maxed out)
- Quarterly virtual cohort of new $49 customers (community-led growth)
- Refund-and-reapply play: refunded customers get a personal note + invite to come back in 3 months
