## Section 4: Secret #10, The Four Core Stories

A complete belief-changing campaign needs four story TYPES, not just one Epiphany Bridge. Each rewrites a specific belief category.

### Story Type 1: The Vehicle Story (why this method works)

> The Machine works because it removes the option to skip. Every founder I talked to skipped the same three things in the same order: naming one real person, writing one real promise, sending one real message. The Machine refuses to let any of them happen out of order. Step 1 will not let you write an offer until you have pinned a real customer. Step 5 will not mark itself complete until 20 outreach actions have been verified in-tool. The 60-day guarantee is enforced by code, not promise. The reason this works where willpower fails is mechanical: the avoidant founder is not asked to overcome avoidance. The tool removes the avoidance option.

### Story Type 2: Internal Beliefs Stories (rewrite Marco's locked false beliefs)

Marco's four locked false beliefs from state.json, each rewritten with a "kinda like" bridge.

| False belief | Rewrite (new belief) | Kinda-like bridge |
|---|---|---|
| "The problem is the product" | "The product was built for no one in particular. The problem is upstream of the product." | Kinda like blaming the car when you never figured out where you were driving. |
| "Idea validation equals customer understanding" | "Validation is theater. Understanding requires a mouth." | Kinda like reading reviews of a restaurant instead of eating there. |
| "More building is progress" | "For an avoidant builder, more building is sophisticated procrastination." | Kinda like reorganizing your desk while the deadline approaches. |
| "I can reason my way to a customer" | "Customers exist outside your head. The only way to find them is to leave it." | Kinda like trying to learn to swim from a textbook on the couch. |

Each gets one Reluctant Hero parable (from workbook 01 Section 6 Beat 3) to carry the bridge.

### Story Type 3: External Beliefs Stories (rewrite the excuses)

The objections Marco gives himself for not buying. Each is a story.

| External belief / excuse | Story rewrite |
|---|---|
| "I do not have time for another framework" | The Machine is not a framework. It is a tool. The framework lives in the engine. You answer 3 to 5 questions per step. Time to first paying customer can be 60 days or less. |
| "I have tried customer interviews and they did not help" | You tried interviews. The Machine forces conclusions FROM interviews. The bottleneck was not the asking, it was the synthesizing. |
| "Outreach is not my strength" | It is not anyone's strength. That is why Step 5 generates the message, picks the target, and the tool tracks the send. Strength is irrelevant to a mechanical process. |
| "$49 a month is too much when I am not earning" | $49 a month is two coffees a week to make the difference between $0 and $49+ recurring forever. If The Machine does not produce a paying customer, the $98 comes back. |
| "I cannot risk another product that does not work" | The product already exists. You shipped it. The Machine is the work AROUND it, not another product to build. |
| "I could build this myself in a weekend" | You could build the form. You can't build the Stripe-webhook proof, the Dream 100 picker fed from the locked workbook, the engine pushback, OR the 60-day refund logic in a weekend. And while you're building the tool, you're not running the funnel — which is the exact disease the Machine treats. Added 2026-05-17 from `strategy/dollar-objections.md` Category 5; surfaced by founder-conversation mine on public IH/HN threads ("the underlying data is basically free", "I try to stay as lean as possible, not paying for any SaaS", "I didn't use any fancy AI tools"). |

### Story Type 4: Internal / External Chains (mapped)

Some beliefs chain. The internal belief "I do not have what it takes" produces the external excuse "the product is wrong." Breaking the chain requires hitting the internal first, then watching the external dissolve.

Three chains to watch for in copy and onboarding:

1. **Identity to product blame.** "I am not a real founder" produces "the product is broken." Fix: rewrite identity ("you are a Verified Builder, two steps in").
2. **Avoidance to tactic shopping.** "I cannot face the customer" produces "I need more marketing tactics." Fix: rewrite avoidance ("the tool removes the avoidance option, you do not need willpower").
3. **Praise to false signal.** "They said it was great" produces "I just need more traffic to find more people who say it is great." Fix: rewrite signal ("praise is not payment, only a Stripe charge is").

## Section 6: Engine Implications

The engine asks each user to generate one Vehicle Story, four Internal Belief rewrites (drawn from their own pinned false beliefs in Step 1), and three External Belief rewrites (drawn from the user's stated objections in the onboarding Four-Question Close).

| Story type | Engine input | Engine output |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle Story | The Machine's 7-step description + user's case | 1-paragraph Vehicle Story in user's voice |
| Internal Beliefs (4) | User's 4 false beliefs from Machine Step 1 | 4 rewrites with kinda-like bridges |
| External Beliefs (6) | User's answers to the Four-Question Close (engine matches against the 6 locked external beliefs, generates a rewrite per match) | up to 6 objection rewrites |
| Chain map (3) | Engine maps recurring patterns | 3 chain breakers used as inline pushback |

Engine assembles. User answers human questions. Never a form.
