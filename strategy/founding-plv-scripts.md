## PLV1 — "The Door That Opened" (5–7 minutes)

Opportunity / Wow & How. Cold viewer leaves believing the new category exists and is real.

**[On camera, Maryan, no slide background, just a face.]**

For most of my life one door was closed.

I am a marketer. An operator. I have never written a line of production code. Every time I looked at the SaaS economy I saw a club I could not enter, because everything inside it required someone who could write code, and that was not me.

Then in 2026, Lovable and Claude opened that door.

I shipped a real AI product in two weeks. Then another. Then a dozen. I am not exaggerating. I built things that actually worked, in production, that I could not have written a single function of by hand. The shipping part felt like magic.

What came after did not.

I would launch, open Stripe, and watch a line lie flat. Twelve users one month, fourteen the next. Two paying, then one, then two again. I told myself it was the product. I rebuilt the onboarding. I rewrote the landing page. I bought a course on positioning. I went embarrassingly deep into SEO so I would not have to look at the flat line.

Then I sat down to talk to ten other founders who had also shipped with AI tools. And every single one of them told me my own story back, almost word for word. The shipping part was magic. The Stripe part was a wall.

That is when the second door opened. The one I want to talk to you about.

**[Cut to a slide or simple text overlay: "The bottleneck moved."]**

For thirty years the bottleneck of small software was the building. If you could build it, you had a shot. Now AI tools have removed that bottleneck. Anyone who can describe a problem clearly can ship the thing that solves it.

But nobody moved the OTHER bottleneck. The work that turns a shipped product into a paying customer. The naming-one-real-person work. The writing-one-real-promise work. The sending-one-real-message work.

That work hasn't moved an inch. And now it sits, fully exposed, in front of every non-engineer who just shipped something with Lovable or Claude.

I built a tool that does that work. With you, not for you. It is called The Playbook. There are seven steps. Every one of them is verified inside the software so you cannot fake your way through. The promise is your first paying customer in 60 days. Verified by your own Stripe. Or you do not pay.

I am opening it to 50 founders on [DATE]. Not 50 ever. Just 50 in the founding cohort. After that the door is still open, but the founding bonuses are not.

In the next video I will show you HOW the Playbook works. The seven steps, the engine, the one step where every other tool quits and what mine does instead.

That video drops Wednesday.

See you then.

— Maryan

## PLV2 — "How the Playbook Actually Works" (8–10 minutes)

Transformation / Education. Teach a piece of the framework. Show the product. Build real understanding, not hype.

**[On camera open, 30 seconds.]**

Welcome back. Today I show you the Playbook, and the one step where every other tool quits.

**[Cut to screen recording of the live product `/playbook/step/1`.]**

The Playbook has seven steps. They go in order. You do not get to step 4 until you have finished step 3. The engine refuses to advance. That is not a UX choice. That is the entire mechanism.

**Step 1: Pin Your Dream Customer.** The engine asks you who you are building for. If you type "founders," it pushes back. "Founders is a category, not a person." It will not take vague answers. I'll show you it doing this to me, live.

**[Screen recording: type "founders" into Step 1. Engine pushes back. Type the real specific avatar. Engine accepts.]**

That pushback is the entire value of step 1. You cannot reason your way past the engine. It is harder to lie to a piece of software than to your own notebook.

**Step 2: Build Your Offer.** Four questions. The engine assembles the headline, the stack, the guarantee, the 10x value math. It will not let you skip a guarantee. It will not let you publish a stack worth less than 10x your price. The skeptic in you will love this constraint, because it produces an offer you can defend.

**[Brief screen recording of Step 2 in action.]**

**Step 3: Define Your Attractive Character.** This is where most founders pre-launch get stuck — they don't have a voice yet. The engine asks two or three origin questions and recommends an identity type. Reluctant Hero, Leader, Adventurer, Reporter. I have never met a marketing tool that does this. I built it because I needed it.

**Step 4: Write the Copy.** The engine has now seen your customer, your offer, your voice. It writes your homepage, your $1 sales page, your headline rotation, your soap-opera emails. You edit. You don't author from scratch.

**Steps 5, 6, and 7.** Outreach. Conversion. Verification. This is where every other marketing tool I have ever seen quits.

**[Voice tightens. This is the load-bearing moment.]**

Step 5 is the one. Step 5 is where you stop reading and start sending. Step 5 is the step you have been avoiding for six months.

Every funnel coach has a video about outreach. They all teach the framework. None of them stand next to you while you actually do it. The Playbook does. The engine writes the message. It picks the target from your Dream 100. You post the message yourself, in the real platform, and you paste the link back into the tool. The tool fetches the link and verifies you did the thing. You cannot get to step 6 without it.

That is the framework-into-the-engine principle. The framework is in the software, not on you. Marco — that's the avatar — Marco does not have to remember a framework. Marco answers human questions and the engine does the framework lifting.

**Step 6: Convert.** The OTO. The follow-up. The mini-closes.

**Step 7: Verify.** Stripe webhook fires. The engine sees a new charge land on YOUR account. The badge unlocks. The verification is mechanical. There is no self-reporting. There is no "feels like." There is a paying customer or there is not.

That is the Playbook.

In the next video I will show you what it looks like to actually own this thing. The Verified Builder identity. The manifesto. The guarantee mechanic with the actual refund button on the screen. And I will tell you when the door opens.

See you Sunday.

— Maryan

## Production notes

- **Camera:** phone is fine. 1080p. Decent lighting (window, daytime). Lavalier mic ($30 on Amazon) is the only required upgrade — laptop mic is the single biggest deliverability killer.
- **Hosting:** Mux or Cloudflare Stream. NOT YouTube — YouTube auto-plays competitor videos at the end and ruins the funnel. Vimeo unlisted works as a Plan B.
- **Length discipline:** PLV1 hard cap at 7 minutes. PLV2 hard cap at 10. PLV3 hard cap at 12. Cut. Then cut again. The skeptic avatar leaves at minute 8 if you have not gotten to the point.
- **Captions:** open captions (burned-in), not closed. Half the audience watches with sound off.
- **Replays:** all three remain available on `/founding/v1`, `v2`, `v3` through cart-close. After cart-close, the pages 404 or redirect to `/starter`. The videos themselves stay archived for legal/refund purposes.
