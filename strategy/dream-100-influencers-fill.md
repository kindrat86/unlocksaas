These 10 candidates fill influencer slots 31–40 in `dream-100.csv`. Selection criteria applied: each person (a) publicly serves the indie-hacker / micro-SaaS / non-engineer-or-vibe-coder audience on at least one platform Marco actually uses (X, YouTube, podcast, or newsletter); (b) has a verified audience of 10K+ on a primary channel; (c) is not selling a competing $49/mo "get your first customer" SaaS; (d) is reachable via DM, podcast pitch, newsletter sponsorship, or public email; and (e) leans toward post-launch operators or AI-builder enablement rather than seed-stage VC, enterprise SaaS, agency, or freelance audiences.
