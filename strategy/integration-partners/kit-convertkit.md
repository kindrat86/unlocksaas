## §1 — Kit, in one paragraph

Kit is the creator-newsletter platform. Already chosen as our marketing-ESP vendor (locked 2026-05-17 per `project_unlocksaas_infra.md`), with activation deferred until 100 list subscribers. Kit has a Creator Marketplace, sponsorship inventory, and a strong publish-to-creators editorial team. The integration argument is structurally clean: we are already a Kit customer; we already recommend Kit in our member onboarding; both are publicly stateable; the partnership formalizes what's already true.

## §2 — Why Kit specifically

| Dimension | Kit |
|---|---|
| Vendor relationship already exists | YES (locked vendor) |
| Audience overlap with Marco | Medium-high (creators launching products is a Marco-adjacent ICP) |
| Creator Marketplace precedent | YES — Kit publishes a tools-for-creators marketplace |
| Co-marketing speed | Fast — fewer organizational gates than Stripe or Lovable |
| Public-statement mutual benefit | Direct (both sides can claim each other publicly) |

Kit's audience is *creators with lists* who are then trying to launch products. Marco is *creators who shipped products* and now need lists. These are bookends of the same journey — the integration argues UnlockSaaS as "what happens after the creator launches the product their list wasn't quite ready for."

| 5 | "What's the timeline?" | "Listing review on your timeline; co-marketing piece on a 4-week scope from kickoff; public reciprocal page within 7 days of kickoff." |
