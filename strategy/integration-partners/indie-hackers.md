## §1 — Indie Hackers, in one paragraph

Indie Hackers is the canonical bootstrapped-founder community. Their members ARE the Marco avatar. The newsletter, the long-form posts, and the daily community feed are where post-launch pre-revenue founders go to read about each other's failures and small wins. Courtland Allen and Channing Allen run editorial; the community surface is where partnerships actually happen.

---

## §2 — Why Indie Hackers specifically

| Dimension | IH score |
|---|---|
| Concentration of Marco-shaped founders | Highest of any single community |
| Editorial-product crossover precedent | Yes (Stripe acquired IH; product / editorial / community blend) |
| Founder presence already established | YES — `dream-100-outreach.md` §1 cadence already prescribes weekly IH long-forms |
| Brand alignment (anti-hype, real-talk, evidence-based) | Excellent — IH's editorial tone matches the Reluctant Hero voice precisely |
| Affiliate / paid relationship precedents | Newsletter sponsorships ($), Sponsored Posts ($$), Podcast ads ($$) — all priced |

The integration argument has two doors: (1) editorial co-brand (free, slower, deeper), (2) paid sponsorship (faster, monetized for IH, less editorial control for us). The pitch in §4 leads with the editorial door and offers paid as the fallback.

## §5 — Asset(s) we propose

### Asset 1 — Co-branded Founder Diagnostic

`indiehackers.com/diagnostic` (preferred) or `unlocksaas.com/from/indie-hackers` (fallback if IH doesn't want sub-path).

### Asset 2 — IH-co-branded Verified Builder badge

Variant of the existing Verified Builder badge OG image with the IH logo alongside ours. Public on `/builder/[slug]` for any IH-routed customer.

### Asset 3 — Member discount code mechanic

A code (e.g., `IH-VERIFIED`) at $1 Starter checkout that flags the customer as IH-sourced in Stripe metadata, triggers the IH-co-branded badge, and surfaces them in the IH-co-branded /builders/ih sub-page.

### Asset 4 — 3 case-study IH posts

After the integration goes live, we draft 3 long-form posts on the IH platform documenting the integration's results — IH-branded, by us, published on IH.

## §6 — Value-exchange math

| IH gets | Value |
|---|---|
| Member-facing diagnostic feature their community has asked for | Feature their product team would otherwise have to build |
| 3 long-form posts documenting real member wins | Editorial content for their community feed |
| Affiliate revenue option (TBD per terms) | Direct revenue if structured as paid |
| Member discount that signals "your community gets special treatment" | Retention signal for IH's own membership product |

| UnlockSaaS gets | Value |
|---|---|
| Direct distribution to the largest concentrated Marco audience | High-intent traffic at scale |
| IH-co-branded badge as identity-anchored social proof | Stronger trust transfer than our standalone badge |
| Permanent IH community feature | Compounds over years |
| Long-form posts on IH (Brunson rule: traffic you've earned permanent placement for) | Owned-traffic-adjacent |
