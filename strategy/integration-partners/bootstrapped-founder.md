## §1 — The Bootstrapped Founder, in one paragraph

Arvid Kahl publishes a weekly podcast + weekly newsletter for bootstrapped founders. His books *Zero to Sold* and *The Embedded Entrepreneur* are canonical reading for ICP-Marco. Arvid's editorial position is *audience-first* — find your community before you build the product. UnlockSaaS exists for the founder who skipped that step (built first, no audience). The integration argument is direct: "Arvid's framework, retrofit for non-engineers who shipped before they had an audience."
