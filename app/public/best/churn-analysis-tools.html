Best SaaS Churn Analysis Tools — 2026

Updated 2026-07-09

You launched. A few people signed up. Then your Stripe dashboard went flat. Churn analysis is how you figure out why — and these are the tools that do it.

1. Baremetrics

Best for: Stripe-native MRR/ARR dashboards. Clean UI, real-time metrics, dunning (Recover).

Pricing: From $129/mo.

Weakness: No due diligence or risk-scoring for acquisition targets. Pure analytics — not a growth tool.

Indie verdict: Excellent if you have $1K+ MRR and want clean dashboards. Overkill before revenue.

2. ChartMogul

Best for: Multi-source revenue analytics. Connects Stripe, Braintree, Recurly, and manual invoices.

Pricing: Free for under $10K MRR. Then from $100/mo.

Weakness: Setup takes longer than Baremetrics — multi-source = multi-config.

Indie verdict: Best free tier in the category. Start here if you're pre-$10K MRR.

3. ProfitWell (Paddle)

Best for: Free retention analytics + benchmarking. Now owned by Paddle.

Pricing: Free for analytics. Paid for retention tools.

Weakness: Paddle acquisition means the roadmap is shifting toward Paddle's payment ecosystem.

Indie verdict: The free retention analytics are genuinely useful. The Paddle lock-in is the trade-off.

4. Unlock SaaS Diagnostic (Free)

Best for: Pre-revenue founders diagnosing why their Stripe line is flat — wrong person, weak offer, or weak belief. Not a dashboard. A diagnostic.

Pricing: Free. 90 seconds.

Verdict: You don't need a $129/mo dashboard when you have zero revenue. Start with the free diagnostic to find the real problem.
