Best SaaS Pricing Tools — 2026

Updated 2026-07-09

Your SaaS needs a way to charge money. These are the tools — from simple payment links for a $1 tripwire, to full subscription management for a $49/mo SaaS.

1. Stripe Payment Links

Fee: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction.

Setup time: Under 1 hour (Stripe account + one link).

Best for: Any SaaS that needs to charge $1–$500. Works with your existing Stripe account — no code needed for the first charge.

Indie verdict: Start here. Get your first dollar. You can build the full subscription billing later.

2. Lemon Squeezy

Fee: 5% + $0.50 per transaction.

Setup time: Under 30 minutes.

Best for: Founders who want a merchant of record — Lemon Squeezy handles global tax (VAT, GST) so you don't have to.

Weakness: Higher fees than Stripe. No subscription management as flexible as Stripe Billing.

3. Paddle

Fee: 5% + $0.50 per transaction.

Setup time: 1-2 days (approval process).

Best for: SaaS making $10K+/mo who want MoR + subscription management + global tax compliance in one platform.

Weakness: Approval process. Higher fees. Overkill before $1K MRR.

4. Gumroad

Fee: 10% flat (free tier: 0% + 10%).

Setup time: Under 30 minutes.

Best for: Digital products, courses, ebooks — NOT recurring SaaS subscriptions.

The Pricing Ladder for Indie Founders

$0–$500 MRR: Stripe Payment Links (cheapest, fastest).
$500–$10K MRR: Stripe Billing (subscriptions) or Lemon Squeezy (if you want MoR).
$10K+ MRR: Paddle (MoR + subscription + tax = one platform).

Start with what gets you to the first dollar fastest. Upgrade when the tool becomes the bottleneck.
