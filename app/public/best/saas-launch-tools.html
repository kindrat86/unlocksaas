Best SaaS Launch Tools in 2026

Updated 2026-07-09

Every indie SaaS founder has asked: "Which tools do I actually need to launch?" Here's the honest answer — ranked by how fast you go from zero to a Stripe-verified first paying customer.

1. Stripe — Payment Infrastructure

Why it's #1: You can't get paid without a payment processor. Stripe's Payment Links lets you charge customers before you write a single line of backend code. Test mode → live mode in under an hour.

Founder-friendly detail: No monthly fee. You pay 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction. The first dollar is the hardest — Stripe charges you nothing until someone pays you.

Alternatives: Paddle (MoR for global tax), Lemon Squeezy (simpler, higher fees).

2. Vercel — Deployment

Why it's #2: You need a live URL. Vercel deploys Next.js apps in seconds from GitHub. Free hobby tier includes SSL, CDN, analytics. You ship code → Vercel handles the rest.

Founder-friendly detail: Free tier covers everything a solo founder needs. Pay when you need team features or higher limits.

Alternatives: Netlify, Railway, Fly.io (for non-JS stacks).

3. Supabase — Database + Auth

Why it's #3: You need to store user data. Supabase gives you Postgres, auth, realtime, and storage — the full backend — with a generous free tier and no vendor lock-in (it's just Postgres).

Founder-friendly detail: Free tier: 500MB database, 50,000 monthly users, 2GB storage. Scales when you do.

Alternatives: Neon (serverless Postgres), Firebase (NoSQL, Google lock-in).

4. Resend — Transactional Email

Why it's #4: You need to email your customers. Resend delivers onboarding, receipts, password resets. Modern API, React email templates, 100 emails/day free.

Founder-friendly detail: The free tier is designed for indie founders — 100 emails/day covers all transactional needs before you hit revenue.

5. PostHog — Product Analytics

Why it's #5: You need to know what users actually do. PostHog tracks pageviews, sessions, feature usage, and conversions — self-hostable, open-source, generous free tier (1M events/month).

Founder-friendly detail: Self-host for free forever. Cloud-hosted free tier: 1 million events/month. You won't hit that before revenue.

Stripe + Vercel + Supabase + Resend + PostHog = a complete indie SaaS stack for under $50/month total in the free tiers. You only pay when you have paying customers.
