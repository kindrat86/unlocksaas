Onboarding is where revenue happens

The average SaaS loses 40–60% of trial users who never experience the product's core value. Onboarding tools help you guide users to their "aha moment" faster — the point where they understand why your product is worth paying for. UnlockSaaS's curriculum teaches the 3-email onboarding sequence that converts trial users at 2× the industry average.

The UnlockSaaS onboarding framework

Our curriculum teaches a 3-part onboarding system: (1) a welcome email delivered 60 seconds after signup with the single most valuable action the user can take; (2) a 2-minute Loom welcome video embedded in the product; (3) a 3-day "success check-in" email asking what they tried and what blocked them. Together, this framework typically doubles trial-to-paid conversion.
