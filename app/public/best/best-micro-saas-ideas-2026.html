What makes a good micro-SaaS idea in 2026

A viable micro-SaaS idea meets three criteria: (1) it solves a specific, painful problem for a well-defined audience you can reach; (2) the problem is acute enough that people pay to solve it; (3) the market is large enough to support $5K–$20K MRR but too small for venture-backed startups to target. These "too small for VC, too profitable to ignore" niches are where solo founders thrive.

Idea categories that consistently work

Spreadsheet replacementsAny industry where people manage critical workflows in Excel. Example: a specialized CRM for wedding photographers, a dispatch tool for HVAC companies.
API wrappers / integrationsConnect two tools that don't talk to each other. Example: sync Shopify orders to QuickBooks for a specific industry vertical.
Compliance / reporting automationIndustries with regulatory paperwork that nobody wants to do. Example: automated OSHA reporting for construction companies.
Marketplace / directory nichesA paid directory for a specific profession. Example: a directory of ghostwriters with verified portfolios.
Browser extensions for power usersA Chrome extension that adds a missing feature to an existing platform. Example: advanced search filters for LinkedIn Sales Navigator.
