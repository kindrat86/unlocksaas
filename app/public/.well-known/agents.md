## Agent-Callable Tools

### `get_churn_cost`

Calculate the cost of SaaS churn based on MRR, churn rate, and CAC.

### `get_ltv`

Calculate customer lifetime value (LTV) for a SaaS business from ARPU, gross margin, and churn rate.

### `project_revenue`

Project SaaS revenue 12 months forward from current MRR and growth assumptions.
