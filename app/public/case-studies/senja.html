How Senja Hit $100K MRR as a Solo Founder

Case Study

Senja builds testimonial collection and social proof widgets. What started as a solo founder solving his own problem grew into a $100K+ MRR SaaS with a team of under 10. Here's what worked.

The Story

The founder, Wilson, was building another SaaS and needed to collect testimonials. Every existing tool was either too expensive ($50+/mo), too complex, or didn't support the formats he needed (video, text, Twitter embeds). He built a simple testimonial collector in a weekend and launched it on Product Hunt. It hit #1 Product of the Day.

What Worked

  1. Free tool funnel: Senja built 33 free tools — testimonial generators, social proof templates, review widgets. Each tool captures an email and upsells to the paid plan. By the time a user signs up for Senja Pro, they've already used 3-5 free tools. The sale is warm.
  2. "Powered by Senja" viral loop: Every testimonial widget displays a small "Powered by Senja" badge. Customers embed these on their own websites — turning every customer into a distribution channel. This single mechanic drove an estimated 40% of new signups.
  3. Product Hunt as a growth engine: Senja launched on Product Hunt early and kept launching — new features, new tools, new templates — earning multiple #1 badges over 2 years. Product Hunt is not a one-time launch. It's a repeatable acquisition channel.

Key Numbers

  • Revenue: $100K+ MRR
  • Team: Under 10
  • Launched: 2022
  • 33 free tools driving top-of-funnel
  • Free-to-paid conversion: 3-5% (industry average: 2-4%)

What Indie Founders Should Steal

Build one free tool per month. It doesn't need to be complex — Senja's testimonial text generator is 50 lines of code. Each free tool targets one micro-search-intent that brings 50-200 visitors/month.

Make your product display itself. If your product has a public-facing component (widgets, badges, embedded content), make it self-distributing. The "Powered by" badge is the most underused growth mechanic in indie SaaS.
