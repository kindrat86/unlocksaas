The MarTech-Specific Playbook

Content is your product's first sale: MarTech buyers discover tools through content — blog posts, comparison pages, "best [category] tools" lists. The playbook's Dream Customer step helps MarTech founders identify which content topics their exact buyer is searching for right now.

Free tools convert: A free email subject line tester, a free headline analyzer, a free SEO audit — MarTech buyers expect a free taste. The playbook encourages building one free micro-tool (under 24 hours of development) as the top-of-funnel entry point.

Positioning against incumbents: "You're not competing with HubSpot. You're competing with 'doing it manually' or 'not doing it at all.'" This positioning frame is the MarTech founder's biggest unlock.

MarTech Benchmarks

  • Average customer acquisition cost (CAC): $200-$1,000 (content-driven founders on the low end).
  • Content marketing ROI: MarTech companies that publish weekly content see 3-5x more organic traffic after 12 months.
  • Churn: Higher than average (5-10% monthly) — buyers switch tools frequently. Retention content matters as much as acquisition content.
