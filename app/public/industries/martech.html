Launching a MarTech SaaS

Building a marketing technology SaaS products requires understanding the unique acquisition channels, pricing dynamics, and customer behaviors in this space. MarTech customers have specific expectations around ROI tracking and ease of use.

Key considerations for MarTech SaaS

  • Customer acquisition: MarTech buyers are reached through content marketing, SEO, and peer recommendations
  • Pricing models: Tiered pricing based on contacts or usage for martech
  • Sales cycle: Medium, driven by budget cycles for martech
  • Churn drivers: Low adoption or poor ROI tracking for martech
