Launching a DevTools SaaS — The Developer-First Playbook

Developer tools sell differently. Developers don't read landing pages — they read documentation. They don't book demos — they test your API. The Unlock SaaS playbook adapts to this reality.

The DevTool-Specific Playbook

Your docs are your landing page: A devtool founder's Dream Customer doesn't read a sales page. They `curl` your API. The playbook's "write one real message to one real person" step for devtools is: "send one real API key with a 2-line usage example that solves their exact problem."

Pricing for developers: Developers hate opaque pricing. The playbook's pricing step for devtools is: "pick a generous free tier, a clear paid tier at 10x the free limit, and show both on the pricing page with no asterisks."

The first paying customer: In devtools, the first paying customer is often someone who hit the free tier limit and paid to stay. The playbook's job is to get them to hit that limit — by making the free tier genuinely useful, not crippled.

DevTool Benchmarks

  • Free-to-paid conversion: 2-8% (highly dependent on free tier generosity).
  • Median time to first paying customer: 30-90 days (faster than general SaaS if the free tier is right).
  • Documentation-driven acquisition: 40-60% of paying devtool customers come from docs/API discovery, not marketing.
