What should a SaaS launch checklist include

Bottom Line A comprehensive SaaS launch checklist covers: pre-launch validation (customer interviews, waitlist), product readiness (MVP, onboarding, billing), marketing assets (landing page, content, social proof), and launch day execution (community posts, email blast, press outreach).

Pre-Launch (4-8 weeks before)

Complete 20-30 customer interviews. Build a waitlist landing page with 100+ signups. Pre-sell to 5-10 founding members. Set up analytics (PostHog/Mixpanel) and CRM (HubSpot). Create your launch list of communities, newsletters, and journalists.

Product Readiness (2-4 weeks before)

MVP with core workflow complete. User onboarding flow tested with 5 beta users. Billing setup (Stripe) tested end-to-end. Error monitoring (Sentry) configured. Help documentation for the top 10 questions.

Marketing Assets (1-2 weeks before)

Landing page with clear value proposition, pricing, and social proof. 3-5 blog posts addressing your target customers' problems. Case study or testimonial from beta users. Product demo video (2-3 minutes). Launch announcement drafted for each platform.

Launch Day Execution

Post on 2-3 relevant communities (Reddit, Hacker News, niche forums). Send email to your waitlist. Reach out to 5-10 journalists/bloggers who cover your space. Activate founding members as advocates. Monitor and respond to every comment and question within 1 hour.

Post-Launch (first 30 days)

Ship improvements based on launch feedback weekly. Publish 2-3 follow-up content pieces addressing questions from launch. Reach out to every new signup personally. Build your first case study from a launch customer.

Key Data Points

  • 5-phase launch framework
  • 100+ waitlist target
  • Weekly shipping cadence

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Should I launch on Product Hunt?

    Product Hunt works well for developer tools, productivity apps, and design tools. For B2B SaaS, your target customers are rarely on Product Hunt — focus on industry-specific communities and LinkedIn instead.
