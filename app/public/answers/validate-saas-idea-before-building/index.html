How do you validate a SaaS idea before building anything

Bottom Line Validate a SaaS idea by running 20-30 customer discovery interviews, creating a landing page with a waitlist, pre-selling to 5-10 customers, and measuring willingness-to-pay before writing a single line of code.

The Customer Discovery Interview

Interview 20-30 people in your target market. Don't pitch your solution — ask about their problems. Key questions: 'Tell me about the last time you dealt with [problem].' 'What have you tried?' 'What did those solutions cost you in time/money?' 'What would a perfect solution look like?'

The Waitlist Landing Page

Build a simple landing page describing the problem you solve and the solution. Include a waitlist signup. Drive traffic through your target communities. If you can't get 100+ signups, the problem isn't painful enough or your messaging is wrong.

Pre-Selling: The Ultimate Validation

Once you have waitlist signups, reach out personally to the most engaged ones. Offer them a founding member deal at 50-70% off — but they must pay now, before the product is built. If 5-10 people pay, you have validation. If nobody pays, you've saved yourself months of building the wrong thing.

Red Flags That Mean 'Don't Build'

Nobody will take a 15-minute call to discuss the problem. Waitlist signups are <50 after 2 weeks of promotion. People say 'sounds interesting' but won't commit to a pre-sale. Your target customers are happy with spreadsheets and don't see the problem as painful.

Key Data Points

  • 20-30 customer interviews
  • 100+ waitlist minimum
  • 5-10 pre-sales = validated

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What if I can't find people to interview?

    Start with your network: LinkedIn connections, former colleagues, alumni groups, and relevant online communities. Offer a $25 Amazon gift card for 30 minutes if you're struggling. It's worth the investment to avoid building the wrong product.
