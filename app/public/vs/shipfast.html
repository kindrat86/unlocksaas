What ShipFast does

ShipFast is a Next.js boilerplate for SaaS focused on providing a Next.js + Supabase boilerplate to launch SaaS products fast with auth, payments, and email pre-configured. It is a useful tool for its specific purpose, but it does not replace the business-side execution that UnlockSaaS provides.

How UnlockSaaS differs

UnlockSaaS is not a development tool. It is a SaaS launch toolkit that covers the business mechanics: pricing, customer acquisition, revenue modeling, churn management, and structured launch execution. You would use ShipFast to build your product and UnlockSaaS to launch and grow it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I choose between ShipFast and UnlockSaaS?

No. They solve different problems. Use ShipFast for providing a Next.js + Supabase boilerplate to launch SaaS products fast with auth. Use UnlockSaaS for SaaS launch and growth execution. Many founders use both together.

Does UnlockSaaS include development tools?

No. UnlockSaaS is focused on the business side of SaaS: pricing, distribution, revenue modeling, and launch strategy. For development, use tools like ShipFast, Next.js, or your preferred stack.
