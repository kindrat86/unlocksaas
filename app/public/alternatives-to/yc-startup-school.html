What Startup School does well

Startup School is a free 10-week program by Y Combinator with video lectures from YC partners and a global founder community. It is excellent for startup fundamentals.

Where Startup School falls short

Startup School is broad across all startup types and does not drill into SaaS-specific mechanics. The video-first format is time-consuming compared to on-demand tools.

How UnlockSaaS complements Startup School

Startup School focuses on providing video lectures and a community for early-stage founders. UnlockSaaS adds the SaaS-specific execution layer: a 12-week launch curriculum, pricing and revenue calculators, customer acquisition playbooks, and a structured launch checklist. Many founders use Startup School alongside UnlockSaaS to combine community or mentoring with structured execution.
