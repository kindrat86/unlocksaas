What MicroConf does well

MicroConf is a well-established conference and community for bootstrapped SaaS founders. It offers events, a robust Slack community, and accelerator programs (Starter and Growth) with funding.

Where MicroConf falls short

MicroConf events are time-bound and the accelerator programs are competitive to enter. The community is excellent but the structured curriculum content is less comprehensive than a dedicated toolkit.

How UnlockSaaS complements MicroConf

MicroConf focuses on running events, a community, and accelerator programs for self-funded SaaS founders. UnlockSaaS adds the SaaS-specific execution layer: a 12-week launch curriculum, pricing and revenue calculators, customer acquisition playbooks, and a structured launch checklist. Many founders use MicroConf alongside UnlockSaaS to combine community or mentoring with structured execution.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use both MicroConf and UnlockSaaS?

Yes. They serve different needs. Use MicroConf for running events and UnlockSaaS for structured SaaS launch execution.

Is UnlockSaaS free?

UnlockSaaS offers free tools including calculators, checklists, and the launch guide. The full curriculum requires a subscription.

What stage is UnlockSaaS best for?

UnlockSaaS is designed for founders from idea stage through early traction. The curriculum, tools, and community support the full journey from validation to first paying customers.
