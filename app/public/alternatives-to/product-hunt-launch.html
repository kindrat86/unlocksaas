What Product Hunt Launch does well

A Product Hunt launch generates a spike of traffic and early adopters for your SaaS. It is an important distribution channel and can validate demand quickly.

Where Product Hunt Launch falls short for SaaS founders

Product Hunt is a single launch event, not a sustained customer acquisition strategy. After launch day, traffic drops to near-zero and you still need ongoing distribution, SEO, content marketing, and sales processes. Many founders treat Product Hunt as their entire go-to-market, which leads to the launch spike followed by a revenue plateau.
