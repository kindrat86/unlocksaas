Understanding SaaS launch checklist

A comprehensive SaaS launch checklist covers positioning, pricing, technical readiness, distribution, and post-launch iteration. Most failed launches skip critical steps in at least one of these areas.

Key elements

  1. Validate the problem with 10+ customer interviews
  2. Define your positioning and unique value proposition
  3. Set initial pricing using the pricing calculator
  4. Build the minimum viable product (not less)
  5. Set up analytics from day one
  6. Prepare your launch channels (email list, social, community)
  7. Create support documentation
  8. Test the signup and payment flow end-to-end
  9. Plan your launch day content
  10. Have a post-launch iteration plan ready

Common pitfalls

  • Rushing: Trying to launch before the fundamentals are in place.
  • Copying without understanding: Replicating what competitors do without knowing why.
  • Optimizing prematurely: Tweaking details before validating the core approach.
