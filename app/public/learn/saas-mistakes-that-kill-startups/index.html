Understanding SaaS mistakes that kill startups

Most SaaS failures are preventable. Founders repeat the same mistakes: building before validating, pricing too low, ignoring churn, and treating launch day as the finish line instead of the starting line.

Key elements

  1. Building before validating the problem
  2. Pricing too low to attract customers
  3. Ignoring churn until it is critical
  4. Focusing on features instead of distribution
  5. Treating launch day as the end goal
  6. Not talking to customers regularly
  7. Copying competitor features without understanding why
  8. Underinvesting in onboarding

Common pitfalls

  • Rushing: Trying to launch before the fundamentals are in place.
  • Copying without understanding: Replicating what competitors do without knowing why.
  • Optimizing prematurely: Tweaking details before validating the core approach.
  • Ignoring feedback: Sticking to a plan that is not working.
