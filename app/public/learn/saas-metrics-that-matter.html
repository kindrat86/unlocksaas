Understanding SaaS metrics that matter

SaaS metrics fall into acquisition (MRR, conversion rate), retention (churn, LTV), and efficiency (CAC payback, gross margin) categories. Founders often track vanity metrics like signups while ignoring the metrics that predict revenue.

Key elements

  1. MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
  2. Churn rate (logo and revenue)
  3. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
  4. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
  5. LTV:CAC ratio (aim for 3:1 or better)
  6. Gross margin
  7. Net Revenue Retention
