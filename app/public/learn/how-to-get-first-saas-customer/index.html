Understanding how to get your first SaaS customer

Getting the first SaaS customer is fundamentally different from getting the 100th. Early customers come from direct outreach, personal networks, and niche communities, not from SEO or paid ads.

Key elements

  1. Identify 20 potential customers in your target persona
  2. Reach out personally via email or LinkedIn
  3. Offer free early access in exchange for feedback
  4. Schedule discovery calls to understand their problem
  5. Manually onboard the first 5-10 customers
  6. Ask for testimonials and referrals
  7. Document what works for repeatable acquisition
