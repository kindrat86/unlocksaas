Understanding SaaS pricing strategies

SaaS pricing is one of the highest-leverage decisions a founder makes. Pricing too low leaves revenue on the table and attracts low-value customers. Pricing too high stalls acquisition. The right strategy depends on your value metric, audience, and competitive landscape.

Key elements

  1. Identify your value metric (what customers are paying for)
  2. Research competitor pricing ranges
  3. Choose a pricing model (flat, tiered, per-seat, usage-based)
  4. Set your initial price at the high end of acceptable range
  5. Build a pricing page that communicates value, not just cost
  6. Test pricing with real customers
  7. Review and adjust quarterly
