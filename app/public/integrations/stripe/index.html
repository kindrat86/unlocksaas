How UnlockSaaS integrates with Stripe

Stripe is a payment processing that accept subscription payments, manage billing, handle trials and upgrades, and track MRR through Stripe's billing system. The UnlockSaaS curriculum and tools are designed to work alongside Stripe, helping you set up and optimize your payment processing as part of your SaaS launch.

What you get from the integration

  • Setup guidance: Step-by-step instructions for configuring Stripe for a SaaS product.
  • Best practices: Architecture patterns that scale from your first customer to thousands.
  • Launch checklist alignment: Stripe setup tasks integrated into the broader launch checklist.
  • Revenue tracking: How to connect Stripe metrics to your MRR, churn, and LTV calculations.

Common Stripe setup scenarios

  1. New SaaS setup: Configure Stripe from scratch with optimal settings for subscription billing and growth tracking.
  2. Migrating from another platform: Transfer existing customers, billing data, and configurations to Stripe.
  3. Scaling existing setup: Optimize your current Stripe configuration for higher volume and more complex pricing models.
