Frequently asked questions

Is PostHog free?

Yes. PostHog has a generous free tier (1 million events/month) that is sufficient for most early-stage SaaS products. The open-source version is also free if self-hosted.

Do I need PostHog to use UnlockSaaS?

No. UnlockSaaS is analytics-platform-agnostic. PostHog is recommended because of its free tier and integrated feature set, but the principles apply to any analytics tool.

Can I self-host PostHog?

Yes. PostHog is open-source and can be self-hosted on your own infrastructure. The UnlockSaaS curriculum covers both cloud and self-hosted setup options.
