Unlock SaaS + Supabase

Supabase provides the backend for indie SaaS products — Postgres database, authentication, real-time subscriptions, and file storage. The playbook assumes you already shipped your SaaS. Supabase is the most common backend among the founders who use the playbook.

How They Work Together

Auth: Supabase Auth handles magic-link login, OAuth (Google, GitHub), and session management. The playbook's customer-acquisition steps don't require auth — but your product does. Supabase gives you production auth in under 30 minutes.

Database: Your SaaS stores user data, subscriptions, and activity in Postgres (via Supabase). The playbook's diagnostic reads your Stripe data — Supabase stores everything else.

Free Tier: 500MB database, 50,000 monthly active users, 2GB file storage, 5GB bandwidth. This covers every indie SaaS from zero to $10K MRR.

Why Developers Choose Supabase

  • Open Source: You can self-host. No vendor lock-in. The cloud version is just managed Supabase.
  • Postgres: Real SQL. Real migrations. Real indexes. Not a proprietary query language.
  • Row-Level Security: Auth-based data access rules — defined once in SQL, enforced everywhere.
  • Realtime: WebSocket subscriptions for live updates — chat, dashboards, collaborative editing — built into the free tier.
