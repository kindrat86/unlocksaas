Unlock SaaS + Stripe

The playbook guides you to a Stripe-verified first paying customer. Stripe handles the payment; the playbook handles everything else — naming the customer, writing the promise, designing the offer.

How They Work Together

Step 1: The playbook helps you identify your Dream Customer — one real person, not a persona.

Step 2: You write a specific promise to that person and price it.

Step 3: You create a Stripe Payment Link for that offer. No code. No checkout page. Just a URL.

Step 4: You send one real message to that one real person — with the payment link.

Step 5: Stripe processes the charge. You see the charge in your dashboard. The playbook's promise (refund if no customer in 60 days) is backed by Stripe's automated refund logic.

Why Stripe Over Alternatives

  • Speed: Payment Links go from zero to live in under an hour. No code, no Stripe API, no webhook setup required for the first charge.
  • Test Mode: The playbook's first-customer exercises use Stripe test mode — you practice with fake charges ($1) before going live.
  • Automated Refunds: The 60-day money-back guarantee is enforced by Stripe automation, not manual refunds — zero trust required from the customer.
  • Real Verification: A Stripe charge ID is the only proof the playbook accepts for "I got my first paying customer." No self-reported wins.
