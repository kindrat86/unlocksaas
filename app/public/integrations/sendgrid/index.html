Frequently asked questions

Is SendGrid free?

SendGrid has a free tier (100 emails/day) that is sufficient for early-stage SaaS. Paid plans start at $19.95/month for higher volumes.

SendGrid vs Resend: which should I use?

Resend is a newer, developer-focused option with React email templates. SendGrid has more features, better deliverability tools, and longer track record. Either works with UnlockSaaS — the curriculum covers both.

Do I need a separate marketing email tool?

For early-stage SaaS, SendGrid handles both transactional and marketing emails. As you grow, you may want a dedicated marketing tool like Loops or ConvertKit for complex sequences. UnlockSaaS covers when and how to make that transition.
