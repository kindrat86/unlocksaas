Why look beyond Notion?

Notion is an all-in-one workspace for docs, wikis, and project management. It is widely used by startups for internal documentation, roadmapping, and lightweight databases.

Notion is a document tool, not a launch toolkit. It has no built-in SaaS-specific launch curriculum, pricing calculators, churn analysis, or revenue projection tools. You can build templates for these in Notion, but they lack the structured guidance and community that a dedicated SaaS launch platform provides.
