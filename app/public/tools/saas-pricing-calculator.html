Frequently Asked Questions

How should I price my SaaS product?

Use value-based pricing: charge 10-30% of the value you deliver. If your SaaS saves a customer $500/month, charge $50-150/month. Never price based on your costs alone — price based on the customer's perceived value.

Should I offer a free tier?

Yes, but make it a true free tier (not a trial). A free tier that solves a real micro-problem drives organic traffic, word-of-mouth, and upsell opportunities. The paid tier should offer 10x the value of free.

How do I know if my SaaS price is too low?

If fewer than 5% of users complain about price, your price is likely too low. If more than 30% complain, it may be too high. The sweet spot: 10-20% of prospects object to price — they see the value but feel the stretch.
