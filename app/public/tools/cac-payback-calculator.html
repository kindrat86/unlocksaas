Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good CAC payback period for SaaS?

Under 12 months for bootstrapped SaaS. Under 18 months for VC-backed with runway. The best SaaS companies achieve under 6 months. Longer payback means you need more upfront capital to fund growth.

How do I reduce CAC payback?

Three levers: (1) Increase ARPU — raise prices or upsell. (2) Reduce CAC — improve organic acquisition, content, SEO. (3) Improve gross margin — reduce hosting/infrastructure costs. The fastest lever is usually raising prices.

What is the relationship between CAC payback and LTV?

CAC payback measures how quickly a customer covers their acquisition cost. LTV measures total revenue over the customer's lifetime. A good SaaS has LTV:CAC of 3:1 or higher AND payback under 12 months.

About the CAC Payback Period calculator

The CAC Payback Period calculator measures how many months it takes for a customer's gross profit to repay the cost of acquiring them. This metric is critical for understanding cash flow efficiency and whether your SaaS can scale sustainably.

How to use it

Enter your Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Average Revenue Per User (ARPU), and gross margin. The calculator outputs the number of months until a customer becomes profitable.

The formula

cac_payback_months = cac / (arpu * gross_margin)

Why customer acquisition cost matters for SaaS

Tracking customer acquisition cost is essential because it directly impacts your growth trajectory and valuation. SaaS investors and acquirers scrutinize this metric because it reveals whether your business model is sustainable. Founders who actively manage customer acquisition cost consistently outperform those who only check it quarterly.
