Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good LTV for SaaS?

A LTV above $300 is healthy for consumer SaaS. For B2B SaaS, $1,000+ is typical. The key ratio is LTV:CAC — aim for 3:1 or higher. If your LTV is under $100, focus on reducing churn or raising prices before spending on acquisition.

How do you calculate LTV for SaaS?

LTV = (ARPU x Gross Margin) / Monthly Churn Rate. For example: $49 ARPU, 80% margin, 5% monthly churn = ($49 x 0.80) / 0.05 = $784 LTV. Simple and accurate for early-stage SaaS.

Should I use simple or cohort-based LTV?

Use simple LTV (ARPU x margin / churn) when you have under 100 customers and uniform pricing. Switch to cohort-based LTV when you have multiple pricing tiers, expansion revenue, or 500+ customers where averages mislead.

About the Customer Lifetime Value calculator

The LTV calculator measures the total revenue a customer generates over their entire relationship with your SaaS. LTV is essential for determining how much you can spend to acquire a customer (CAC) while remaining profitable.

How to use it

Enter your ARPU, gross margin, and monthly churn rate. The calculator outputs customer lifetime value and the LTV:CAC ratio benchmark.

The formula

ltv = (arpu * gross_margin) / monthly_churn_rate

Why LTV matters for SaaS

Tracking LTV is essential because it directly impacts your growth trajectory and valuation. SaaS investors and acquirers scrutinize this metric because it reveals whether your business model is sustainable. Founders who actively manage LTV consistently outperform those who only check it quarterly.
