Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good churn rate for SaaS?

A monthly churn rate under 3% is excellent (top quartile). 3-5% is healthy for early-stage SaaS. 5-7% is the industry median. Above 8% is critical and means your product or onboarding has a fundamental problem.

How is SaaS churn calculated?

Churn rate = (customers lost during period / customers at start of period) x 100. Customers lost = start customers + new customers - end customers. This accounts for growth during the period.

What is the difference between gross and net churn?

Gross churn only counts lost customers. Net churn subtracts expansion revenue (upgrades, cross-sells) from lost revenue. A SaaS with 5% gross churn but 3% expansion MRR has 2% net revenue churn.

About the Churn Rate calculator

The Churn Rate calculator measures the percentage of customers who cancel their subscription over a given period. Churn is the silent killer of SaaS businesses because it compounds over time and caps your maximum MRR.

How to use it

Enter the number of customers at the start and end of the period, plus the number of new customers acquired. The calculator outputs your logo churn rate and revenue churn rate.

The formula

churn_rate = (customers_lost / customers_start) * 100

Why customer churn matters for SaaS

Tracking customer churn is essential because it directly impacts your growth trajectory and valuation. SaaS investors and acquirers scrutinize this metric because it reveals whether your business model is sustainable. Founders who actively manage customer churn consistently outperform those who only check it quarterly.
