Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good MRR growth rate for SaaS?

Traction (VC-backed) benchmark is 15%+ MoM early on. For bootstrapped SaaS, 5-10% MoM is strong. The key metric is net revenue retention — if it's above 100%, you grow even without new customers.

How does churn affect MRR growth?

Churn is a leak in your bucket. At 5% monthly churn, you lose 46% of customers per year. Reducing churn from 5% to 3% can double your MRR in 12 months without acquiring a single new customer. Use the churn calculator above to see your numbers.
