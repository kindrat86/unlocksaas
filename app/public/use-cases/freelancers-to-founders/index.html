Frequently asked questions

Should I quit freelancing to focus on my SaaS?

No. Most successful freelancers-turned-founders keep freelancing during the transition. Use freelance income to fund development and reduce pressure. Only go full-time on SaaS when revenue is stable and growing.

How do I convince clients to switch to a subscription?

Position it as an upgrade: 'I have built a platform that gives you faster results at a lower cost.' Show the value, not the pricing change.

What if my SaaS fails?

You still have your freelance business. One advantage of building a SaaS as a freelancer: you have a safety net. The worst case is you learned a lot and go back to freelancing with new skills.
