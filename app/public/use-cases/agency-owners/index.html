Why agency owners use UnlockSaaS

Agency owners transitioning to SaaS face a mindset shift from project-based revenue to recurring revenue. The mechanics of SaaS pricing, churn management, and MRR growth are fundamentally different from client work.

UnlockSaaS bridges this gap with SaaS-specific tools. The pricing calculator helps you model subscription pricing. The revenue projector shows MRR growth trajectories. The curriculum covers the transition from one-time project revenue to recurring SaaS revenue.

What agency owners get from the toolkit

  • Structured launch curriculum: A 12-week path from idea to paying customers, with specific weekly objectives.
  • Pricing calculator: Model subscription pricing based on value delivered, competitor pricing, and customer willingness to pay.
  • Revenue projector: Forecast MRR growth over 12 months based on your assumptions about acquisition and churn.
  • Churn cost calculator: Quantify the revenue impact of churn to prioritize retention work.
  • LTV calculator: Understand customer lifetime value to guide acquisition spend decisions.
  • Launch checklist: Ensure nothing critical is missed before, during, and after launch.
  • SaaS mistakes guide: Learn from common pitfalls that kill early-stage SaaS products.
  • Founder community: Connect with other agency owners for accountability, feedback, and support.

Frequently asked questions

I am a agency owner with no SaaS experience. Is this for me?

Yes. UnlockSaaS is designed for founders at all stages. The curriculum starts from the fundamentals and builds up to advanced SaaS mechanics like churn modeling and pricing strategy.

I already have a SaaS product. Can UnlockSaaS still help?

Absolutely. The calculators, mistakes guide, and customer acquisition playbook are useful at any stage. Many founders use the launch checklist as a post-launch audit to identify gaps in their current go-to-market.

How much time does it require per week?

The curriculum is designed for 5-10 hours per week. You can move faster or slower depending on your schedule. The calculators and tools can be used independently of the curriculum at any time.
