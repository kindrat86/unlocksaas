Frequently asked questions

I have never built a SaaS before. Is this too advanced?

No. UnlockSaaS is designed for first-time founders. The curriculum starts from the fundamentals and does not assume prior SaaS experience. The calculators do the math so you do not have to.

How long until I get my first customer?

The curriculum is designed as a 12-week program, but many founders acquire their first customer before completing it. The speed depends on your execution and how much time you dedicate each week.

What if my SaaS idea is not validated yet?

The curriculum includes idea validation in the first weeks. The pricing calculator and competitive analysis tools help you test whether your idea has market demand before you invest heavily.
