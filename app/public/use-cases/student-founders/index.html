Frequently asked questions

Is it realistic to launch a SaaS while in school?

Yes. Many successful SaaS founders started in college. The key is picking a small, targeted idea and using no-code tools to move fast. Treat it as a learning experience first, a business second.

What if I have no coding experience?

No-code tools like Bubble and Glide let you build without code. Focus on the business skills — pricing, marketing, customer acquisition — that apply regardless of the technical implementation.

How do I balance school and SaaS?

The 12-week curriculum is designed for part-time execution at 5-10 hours per week. Treat your SaaS like an extracurricular: consistent, focused work each week yields real progress over a semester.
