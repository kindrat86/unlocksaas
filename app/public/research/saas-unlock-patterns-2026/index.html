Introduction

How do SaaS companies actually "unlock" paid conversion? Two dominant strategies compete: feature-gating (limit what the free tier can do, but never expire it) and time-limited trials (full-featured access that expires after 7–30 days). Some combine both.

This analysis examines the pricing pages of 15 well-known SaaS products to map the landscape of unlock patterns: which model each uses, trial duration, pricing structure, and estimated conversion rates based on publicly available data from the companies and industry benchmarks.

Methodology

This data was curated from publicly available pricing pages and published conversion benchmarks for each product. Trial durations and pricing were sourced from the companies' official pricing pages as of June 2026. Conversion rates are estimated benchmarks compiled from public investor letters, S-1 filings, published interviews, and industry reports (OpenView, ChartMogul, ProfitWell).

Where a product uses both feature-gating and time-limited trials, it is classified as "feature gated" since that is the primary unlock mechanism. Two products (Slack, GitHub) use a strictly freemium model with no time limit — their conversion rates reflect voluntary upgrade from free to paid.

Data disclaimer: Conversion rates are estimated from publicly available sources, not proprietary internal data. Actual rates vary by customer segment, geography, and sales motion. These figures are directional and should not be used as guaranteed benchmarks for financial modeling.
