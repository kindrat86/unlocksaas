How to Use This Data

If you are building a SaaS in one of these categories, price within 30 percent of the median to avoid being perceived as too cheap (low trust) or too expensive (no trial conversions). The categories with the highest ARR per customer (Analytics, Marketing Automation, Security) are where the real money is per logo. The categories with "High" churn impact (CRM, Project Management) need stronger retention built in from day one.
