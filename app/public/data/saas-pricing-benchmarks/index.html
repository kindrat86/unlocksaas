How This Data Was Collected

Pricing data was compiled from publicly available pricing pages for the top 5 to 10 vendors in each category as of July 2026. Median and range reflect the most common paid plan advertised, excluding enterprise/ custom pricing. Free tier availability is based on whether the market leader in each category offers a permanently free plan with meaningful functionality, not time-limited trials.

Pricing Models Explained

Per user (58%): The customer pays a fixed amount per user per month. CRM, project management, and communication tools dominate here. This model scales with adoption: the more people use the tool, the more the customer pays.

Tiered usage (23%): Pricing scales with volume: contacts, API calls, emails sent, or data processed. Email marketing, analytics, and API platforms use this model. It aligns cost with value but is harder to predict month to month.

Flat rate (19%): One price for everything, sometimes with feature gating across plans. Website builders, course platforms, and no-code tools favor this model. It is the simplest to sell but leaves money on the table with heavy users.

Use This Data to Price Your SaaS

Find your category above. If your price is below the median, you are likely underpricing. If your price is above the range high, you need a clear premium justification (enterprise features, compliance, dedicated support). If your category has a free tier and you do not, you are losing top-of-funnel signups to competitors who convert free users later.
