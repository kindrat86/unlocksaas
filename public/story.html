The story of a builder who shipped 14 SaaS products, watched 12 of them make nothing, and built the playbook that turns shipped products into paying customers in 60 days.

The Pattern That Took 14 Products to See

Between 2021 and 2025, I shipped 14 SaaS products. Voice tools. Compliance checkers. Analytics dashboards. MicroSaaS experiments. Feature-rich, well-designed, technically impressive. Most of them made $0. Two of them made enough to cover server costs. None of them became a business.

I told myself the same story every builder tells: "The product isn't good enough yet." "I need more features." "The market isn't ready." So I added more features. Polished more UI. Shipped more updates. The products got better. The revenue didn't move.

"The turning point wasn't a better product. It was a brutal question: why did products #13 and #14 make money when products #1 through #12 didn't? The products weren't different. The approach was."

— Maryan, founder of Unlock SaaS

Products #13 and #14 made money because I'd stopped treating "shipped" as the finish line. I'd started treating it as the starting line. The real work — finding the customer, framing the offer, setting up the revenue path — only begins after the product exists. That's the work nobody teaches. That's the work Unlock SaaS systematizes.

The guarantee isn't marketing. It's the methodology: if your shipped product doesn't have a paying customer in 60 days, you don't pay. Because the playbook either works or it doesn't. And after 14 products, I know it works.
